Buying guide: how to choose the right 65 inch TV under 1000

What does $1,000 buy in a 65-inch TV?

Under $1,000 you can choose between a very bright Mini LED TV and an entry-level OLED. Mini LED models such as the Hisense U8 and TCL QM7K are the best choice for bright living rooms, sports and HDR. The LG B5 OLED gives perfect blacks for evening movies but is dimmer. Pricier OLEDs like the LG C5 and Samsung S90F are in our full 65-inch TV guide.

Mini LED or OLED?

OLED pixels light themselves, so black is truly black and viewing angles are wide. Mini LED uses thousands of small LEDs behind an LCD panel and gets much brighter, which helps in daylight. If your TV faces windows, pick Mini LED. If you watch mostly in the evening with the lights low, the LG B5 OLED is worth stretching the budget for.

Is 65 inches the right size?

For 4K, a 65-inch TV works best at about 8 to 9 feet from the couch. It is roughly 57 inches wide, so measure your TV stand and check where the feet sit. Wall mounting is often easier at this size; a sturdy mount costs about $50 to $100.

Gaming features to check

For a PS5, Xbox Series X or gaming PC, look for 120Hz or higher with VRR and ALLM. The Hisense U8 and U7 run at 165Hz, the TCL QM6K, QM7K, Hisense U6 and Fire TV Omni Mini-LED at 144Hz, and the LG B5, LG QNED85A, Roku Pro and Samsung QN70F at 120Hz. Most have only two full-speed HDMI 2.1 ports, so count your devices.

Dolby Vision, HDR10+ and smart platforms

TCL, Hisense, Roku and Amazon support both Dolby Vision and HDR10+. LG supports Dolby Vision, and Samsung supports HDR10+ only. Google TV has the biggest app store, Roku OS is the simplest, webOS and Tizen are fast, and Fire TV is best for Alexa homes but shows more ads.

Prices change, check before you buy

Amazon prices on TVs move often, especially around Black Friday. The LG B5 sits right at $999.99, so a small increase can push it over this budget. The Mini LED picks between $450 and $800 leave room for a soundbar, which every TV in this list benefits from.
