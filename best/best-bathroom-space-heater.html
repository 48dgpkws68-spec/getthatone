Buying guide: how to choose the right bathroom space heaters

Bathroom safety comes first

Most space heater manuals say not to use the heater in a bathroom, because water and electricity do not mix. Choose a model sold for bathrooms with an ALCI plug, which cuts power in a fraction of a second on a leakage fault, and look for a splash rating such as IP24 or IP44. Keep any heater at least three feet from the tub, shower and sink, never touch it with wet hands and never use an extension cord.

GFCI outlets and your circuit

US electrical code requires GFCI protection on bathroom outlets, the receptacle with test and reset buttons. Test it before you plug in a heater. A 1,500 watt heater draws about 12.5 amps on 120 V, and bathroom circuits are often shared with a hair dryer or other outlets, so running both can trip the breaker. If that happens, use the heater's low setting or a low-wattage panel.

Portable, wall-mounted or towel warmer?

Small portable ceramic heaters are cheap and heat fast, but they take floor or counter space near water. Wall-mounted heaters stay out of the way and away from splashes, and plug-in models like the Stiebel Eltron CK 150-1 Trend need no electrician. Towel warmers use 60 to 150 watts and mainly keep towels warm and dry, with only a little background heat.

How much power do you need?

Bathrooms are small, so you rarely need more than the usual 1,500 watt maximum of a plug-in heater. As a rule of thumb, 10 watts per square foot is enough for a short warm-up, which means even a 50 square foot bathroom heats in minutes. For all-day background heat in a small room, a 250 watt panel is often enough and far easier on the circuit.

Thermostat and timer save money

Basic heaters only have high and low, so they run at full power until you switch them off. A thermostat holds a set temperature and a timer switches the heater off after your shower, or warms the bathroom before your alarm. App control, as on the Dreo WH517AS, lets you set a weekday schedule. For towel warmers, a smart plug adds the same convenience.

What does a bathroom heater cost to run?

At an average US electricity rate of about 17 cents per kWh, a 1,500 watt heater costs roughly 25 cents per hour on high. Run it 20 minutes twice a day and you spend around 5 dollars a month. A 150 watt towel warmer left on for 8 hours a day costs about 6 dollars a month, and a 60 watt model less than half of that.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best space heater for a bathroom?

Based on our comparison of specs, prices and buyer reviews, the Dreo DR-HSH004A is the best bathroom space heater for most homes. It has an ALCI safety plug, a digital thermostat, a remote and a timer. The Lasko CD08200 is the best budget choice and the Stiebel Eltron CK 150-1 Trend the best wall-mounted heater.

Is it safe to use a space heater in a bathroom?

Only with the right heater and precautions. Choose a model with an ALCI plug or one made for bathrooms, plug it into a GFCI-protected outlet, keep it at least three feet from the tub, shower and sink and never touch it with wet hands. Do not use extension cords and switch the heater off when you leave the room.

What is an ALCI plug?

ALCI stands for appliance leakage current interrupter. It is a safety device built into the plug head that shuts off power within a fraction of a second when it detects a leakage fault, for example after contact with water. It works like a GFCI outlet, but it travels with the heater. Bathroom heaters from Dreo, Lasko and Reaks have one.

Can I use a regular space heater in the bathroom?

It is not recommended. Most regular heaters are not designed for humidity and splashes, and their manuals advise against bathroom use. If you have no other option, plug it into a GFCI outlet, keep it far from water and never leave it running unattended. A heater with an ALCI plug or a splash rating is the safer choice.

Do I need an electrician for a wall-mounted bathroom heater?

Not if you choose a plug-in model. The Stiebel Eltron CK 150-1 Trend, Stelpro ASOA1501PW, Dreo WH517AS and EconoHome panel all plug into a standard 120 V outlet. Many other 1,500 watt wall heaters, such as the Stiebel Eltron CK 15E, must be hardwired on a dedicated circuit, which is a job for an electrician.

Will a towel warmer heat my bathroom?

Only a little. Towel warmers use 60 to 150 watts, compared with 1,500 watts for a space heater, so they mainly keep towels warm and dry and add some background warmth. In a bathroom that already gets heat from the house, that can be enough. In a cold bathroom, combine a towel warmer with a heater.
