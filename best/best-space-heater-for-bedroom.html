The 10 Best Space Heaters for the Bedroom of 2026

It has to be quiet enough to sleep next to, safe enough to run while you are not watching it and smart enough to hold a steady temperature without blasting heat all night. Fanless oil-filled radiators and mica panels are silent, while small ceramic heaters warm up faster but make some noise. Buying guide: how to choose the right space heaters for the bedroom

Quiet matters more than speed

In a bedroom, noise is the deciding factor. Oil-filled radiators and mica panels have no fan, so they are silent apart from a soft click when the thermostat switches. Ceramic and infrared heaters with a fan warm up faster but produce a steady whoosh or hum. If you are a light sleeper, choose a fanless heater and switch it on 30 minutes before bedtime.

How much power do you need?

Every plug-in heater in the US is limited to about 1,500 watts on a standard 120 V outlet, so the differences are in how well they hold the heat. As a rule of thumb, 1,500 watts is enough for a room of about 150 square feet as the only heat source, and more if your central heating does most of the work. A typical bedroom of 120 to 200 square feet is a good match.

Safety at night

Look for tip-over shutoff, overheat protection and ETL or UL certification. Keep the heater at least three feet from the bed, curtains and clothes, plug it directly into a wall outlet and never use an extension cord or power strip. Oil-filled radiators have no exposed glowing elements, which makes them the safest choice for unattended overnight use.

Thermostat, timer and display

A digital thermostat that shows degrees lets you set a precise temperature, so the heater cycles instead of running at full power all night. A timer switches it off after you fall asleep or warms the room before your alarm. Check whether the display can be dimmed or turned off; a bright screen can disturb sleep.

Avoiding dry air

Electric heaters do not remove moisture from the air, but warm air holds more moisture, so relative humidity drops and the room feels dry. Keep the bedroom at a moderate temperature, ventilate briefly each day and use a humidifier if you wake up with a dry throat. A thermostat that avoids overheating the room also helps.

What does a night of heating cost?

At the US average of about 17 cents per kWh, a 1,500 watt heater running at full power costs roughly 25 cents per hour. With a thermostat it usually runs at full power only part of the time, so eight hours in a small, insulated bedroom often costs between 75 cents and $1.50. Lowering the setting by a few degrees saves more than any heater technology.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best space heater for a bedroom?

Based on our comparison of specs, prices and buyer reviews, the De'Longhi Dragon TRD40615T is the best space heater for a bedroom. It is silent, has a digital thermostat with Eco mode and a 24-hour timer. The Amazon Basics oil heater with remote is the best budget choice.

Is it safe to leave a space heater on overnight?

It can be, if you choose a heater with tip-over shutoff, overheat protection and ETL or UL certification, keep it at least three feet from bedding and curtains and plug it directly into a wall outlet. Oil-filled radiators are the safest for overnight use because they have no exposed hot elements. A timer that switches the heater off adds extra peace of mind.

Which space heater makes no noise?

Fanless heaters are silent: oil-filled radiators such as the De'Longhi TRD40615T, Dreo DR-HSH012 and Amazon Basics oil heater, and mica panel heaters such as the De'Longhi HMP1500. You only hear a faint click when the thermostat switches. Ceramic and infrared heaters use a fan and always make some sound.

How much does it cost to run a space heater in a bedroom all night?

A 1,500 watt heater uses 1.5 kWh per hour at full power, about 25 cents at the US average electricity price. With a thermostat cycling the heater, eight hours in a small, insulated bedroom typically costs between 75 cents and $1.50. Your local electricity rate and how cold the room is make the biggest difference.

Does a space heater dry out the air in the bedroom?

A heater does not take water out of the air, but warming the room lowers the relative humidity, so the air feels drier. Keep the temperature moderate, air out the room briefly each day and use a humidifier if you wake up with a dry throat or skin.

Can I plug a space heater into an extension cord?

No. Manufacturers and safety organizations advise plugging space heaters directly into a wall outlet. A 1,500 watt heater draws about 12.5 amps, which can overheat extension cords and power strips. Also avoid sharing the outlet circuit with other high-power appliances.
