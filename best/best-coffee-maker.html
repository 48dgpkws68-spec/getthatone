Buying guide: how to choose the right best coffee maker

Coffee maker or espresso machine?

A drip coffee maker brews a full pot of regular coffee, which is how most Americans drink it at home. An espresso machine makes concentrated shots for lattes and cappuccinos and costs more and takes more skill. If you mostly drink regular coffee, a good drip machine gives far better value.

Why brewing temperature matters

Coffee extracts best between 195 and 205°F. Many cheap machines brew cooler, which makes coffee taste weak or sour. Machines certified by the Specialty Coffee Association, like the OXO Brew, Moccamaster and Breville Precision Brewer, meet strict temperature and brew time standards.

Thermal or glass carafe?

A glass carafe sits on a hot plate that keeps coffee warm but slowly cooks it, making it taste bitter after about an hour. A thermal stainless steel carafe keeps coffee hot for hours without extra heat and tastes fresher. If you drink coffee slowly through the morning, choose thermal.

Pods, grounds or whole beans?

Combo machines like the Ninja DualBrew brew both a pot and single-serve pods. Grind and brew machines grind whole beans fresh, which improves flavor noticeably. If you already own a good burr grinder, a standard drip machine is enough.

Cleaning and descaling

Mineral buildup from hard water slows brewing and affects taste. Descale every one to three months with a descaling solution or a vinegar and water mix, then run two cycles of plain water. Wash the carafe and filter basket after each use, and use filtered water if your tap water is hard.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best coffee maker in 2026?

Based on our comparison of specs, prices and buyer reviews, the OXO Brew 8-Cup is the best coffee maker for most kitchens. It brews at the right temperature, saturates grounds evenly and keeps coffee hot in a thermal carafe. The Cuisinart PerfecTemp 14-Cup is the best value.

Is an expensive coffee maker worth it?

Machines like the Moccamaster or Breville Precision Brewer brew at the ideal temperature and last much longer, which you can taste in the cup. If you drink coffee every day and buy good beans, the difference is worth it. For occasional coffee, a budget machine is fine.

What coffee maker makes the hottest coffee?

The Cuisinart PerfecTemp 14-Cup is designed to brew hotter than typical budget machines. SCA-certified brewers like the OXO Brew and Moccamaster brew between 195 and 205°F, which is ideal for flavor.

How much coffee should I use per cup?

A good starting point is about 2 tablespoons of ground coffee, roughly 10 grams, per 6 ounces of water. Note that a coffee maker cup is usually 5 or 6 ounces, not a full mug.

How often should I clean my coffee maker?

Rinse the carafe and filter basket daily and descale every one to three months, more often with hard water. A sign that it is time is slower brewing or coffee that tastes flat.

What is the best coffee maker for pods and a full pot?

The Ninja DualBrew Pro CFP301 brews both a full carafe from grounds and single cups from K-Cup pods, and adds a milk frother. The Hamilton Beach FlexBrew Trio is a cheaper alternative.
