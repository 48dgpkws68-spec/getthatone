Buying guide: how to choose the right OLED TV under 1000

What does $1,000 buy in an OLED TV?

Under $1,000 you get entry-level OLEDs in 55 and 65 inches, such as the LG B5 and Samsung S85F, or a higher series in a small size, such as the 48-inch LG C5 and Samsung S90F. All of them have the perfect blacks and wide viewing angles OLED is known for. What you do not get is the brightness of flagship models like the LG G5 or Sony BRAVIA 8 II, which are in our full OLED TV guide.

Which size should you choose?

For a living room with the couch 6 to 8 feet away, 55 inches is the sweet spot, and the 55-inch LG B5 leaves about $200 of the budget. At 8 feet or more, the 65-inch B5 is worth stretching for. The 42-inch and 48-inch models suit a bedroom, a small apartment or a desk. They cost more per inch, so only pick them when you need the size.

Is an OLED under $1,000 bright enough?

For evening viewing, yes. OLED pixels switch off completely, so contrast is excellent even at modest brightness. In a room with large windows and daytime viewing, the OLEDs in this budget look dimmer than a Mini LED TV at the same price. If your TV faces a window, close the curtains for movies or look at the Mini LED picks in our guide to the best TVs under $1,000.

Gaming: refresh rate and HDMI 2.1

Every TV here handles 4K at 120Hz with VRR for a PS5 or Xbox Series X. The LG C5 and Samsung S90F go up to 144Hz with a gaming PC. Count the fast ports: the LG B5 and Panasonic Z85 have two HDMI 2.1 ports, while the LG C5 and the Samsung models have four. With one console and a soundbar, two is enough.

Dolby Vision or HDR10+?

LG supports Dolby Vision, the format used by Netflix, Disney+ and Apple TV+. Samsung supports HDR10+ instead and never Dolby Vision; those services then play in regular HDR10. The Panasonic Z85 supports both. The difference is visible mostly in dark scenes on a good panel, so movie fans usually prefer a TV with Dolby Vision.

Prices change, check before you buy

Several picks sit right at $999.99: the 65-inch LG B5, 48-inch and 42-inch LG C5, Samsung S85H and 48-inch Samsung S90F. A small increase pushes them over the limit, and they are cheapest around Black Friday and other sales. The 48-inch and 55-inch LG B5 and the Samsung S85F stay comfortably under $1,000. Watch out for bundles and renewed listings on Amazon.
