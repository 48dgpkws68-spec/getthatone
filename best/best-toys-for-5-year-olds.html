The 10 Best Toys for 5 Year Olds of 2026

Five year olds are ready for toys with a goal: a puzzle to solve, a robot to program, a LEGO set to finish or a scooter to master. They follow simple rules, play games together and love to show what they made. Every toy in this list is rated by its maker for ages 5 and up, so it is challenging without being too hard. We compared ten popular toys for 5 year olds on Amazon, from Botley 2.0 and Rush Hour Junior to LEGO, Snap Circuits and the Razor A3, on price, specs and buyer reviews.

Learning Resources rates Botley 2.0 for ages 5 and up, and it is one of the best known screen-free coding toys. The 78-piece Activity Set includes the robot, a remote programmer, 40 coding cards, 6 double sided boards and stackable obstacles such as cones, balls and gates. Kids lay out a path with the cards, press the matching arrows on the remote and watch Botley drive the route.

Version 2.0 can store sequences of up to 150 steps, move in six directions, follow colored lines and put on a light show. That means a 5 year old starts with three steps and can still find new challenges at 7 or 8. Parents and teachers praise how quickly kids understand cause and effect. Botley takes 3 AAA batteries and the remote 2 AAA, none included, so buy a pack with it. The many small pieces need a storage box.

Razor rates its A-series kick scooters for ages 5 and up, and the A3 is the comfortable model in the line. Compared to the classic Razor A, it has larger wheels and a front suspension that takes the edge off cracks in the sidewalk. The aluminum frame folds in seconds, the handlebars adjust in height and it supports riders up to 143 pounds, so it lasts well into the school years.

A 5 year old moving up from a three wheel scooter needs a few days to learn balancing on two wheels, and many parents start in a quiet driveway or park. If budget matters, the smaller-wheeled Razor A costs less, but buyers say the A3 rolls noticeably smoother and quieter.

Buying guide: how to choose the right toys for 5 year olds

Check the age on the box

Every toy here is rated by its maker for ages 5 and up, or for a range that starts at 5. That rating says the toy matches the skills of a 5 year old, and it also warns about small parts. LEGO, Plus-Plus and game pieces are small, so keep them away from toddlers in the same house.

What 5 year olds are ready for

At 5, most kids can follow simple step by step instructions, play by rules and wait their turn. That opens the door to LEGO sets with instructions, cooperative board games, logic puzzles and first coding toys. They also love to show what they made, so toys with a finished result work well.

Mix thinking toys with active play

A good gift list for a 5 year old has one toy for the brain, like Botley or Rush Hour Junior, one creative toy, like LEGO, Plus-Plus or an art set, and something active, like a scooter. That balance keeps kids busy indoors and outdoors through the winter and spring.

Batteries and extras

Botley 2.0 needs 5 AAA batteries in total and Snap Circuits Beginner 3 AA, none included. The Razor A3 needs a helmet, sold separately. Buy these extras with the gift, so nothing stops the fun on Christmas morning.

Order in time for Christmas

Black Friday is November 27, 2026 and Cyber Monday November 30. Popular LEGO sets and scooters can sell out in December. Order by mid December for delivery before December 25, and earlier if you ship to family in another state.

Gift receipts and returns

Add a gift receipt without prices at checkout. Most items shipped and sold by Amazon in November and December can be returned until the end of January, which helps when two relatives pick the same toy. Check the return window on the product page.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best toy for a 5 year old?

For most 5 year olds, Learning Resources Botley 2.0 is the best choice. It is rated for ages 5 and up and teaches coding without a screen. On a budget, ThinkFun Rush Hour Junior costs about $20 and offers 40 puzzles.

What is a good Christmas gift for a 5 year old?

Popular Christmas gifts for a 5 year old are the Razor A3 scooter, a LEGO City or LEGO Friends set, Snap Circuits Beginner and the cooperative game Outfoxed!. All are rated by the maker for ages 5 and up.

Can a 5 year old build LEGO alone?

Yes, most 5 year olds can build small sets rated 5+, like the LEGO City Yellow Taxi with 122 pieces, with little help. Larger sets rated 6+ or 7+ usually need a parent at this age.

What are good educational toys for a 5 year old?

Botley 2.0 teaches coding, Rush Hour Junior builds logic and planning, Snap Circuits Beginner introduces electronics and Outfoxed! practices deduction and teamwork. All are screen-free.

Is a Razor scooter good for a 5 year old?

Yes. Razor rates its A-series kick scooters, including the A3, for ages 5 and up, with a weight limit of 143 pounds. Always add a helmet and start practicing in a quiet place.

How much should I spend on a toy for a 5 year old?

Great toys for 5 year olds start around $15 for a small LEGO set. Games and art sets cost about $20 to $30, Botley 2.0 about $65 and the Razor A3 about $80.
