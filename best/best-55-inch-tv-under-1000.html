Buying guide: how to choose the right 55 inch TV under 1000

OLED or Mini LED under $1,000?

A $1,000 budget is where the choice gets interesting. OLED, like the LG B5 and Samsung S85F, gives perfect black, no blooming and wide viewing angles, which is ideal for movies in the evening. Mini LED, like the Hisense U8 and TCL QM7K, gets much brighter, which helps in a sunny room and with daytime sports. Watch mostly at night: choose OLED. Bright room or lots of sports: choose Mini LED.

Brightness and your room

Brightness matters more than any spec sheet number when your TV faces windows. The Hisense U8 and U7 are the brightest TVs in this guide, followed by the TCL QM7K. The OLEDs are bright enough for most living rooms in the evening but can look flat in direct afternoon sun. An anti-reflective screen, as on the QM7K, also helps.

Gaming: refresh rate and HDMI 2.1

For a PS5, Xbox Series X or gaming PC, look for a native 120Hz or higher panel with VRR and ALLM. Every TV here qualifies except the Amazon Omni Mini-LED and Samsung U8000F, which are 60Hz. The Hisense U8 and U7 reach 165Hz. Count your HDMI 2.1 ports: most models have two, which covers one console and a soundbar on eARC. The Samsung S85F has four.

Dolby Vision or HDR10+?

Netflix, Disney+ and Apple TV+ mostly stream in Dolby Vision, while Prime Video also uses HDR10+. TCL, Hisense and Amazon support both. LG and Sony support Dolby Vision but not HDR10+, and Samsung supports HDR10+ only. If you watch a lot of Netflix, a TV with Dolby Vision gets the most out of it.

Budget for sound

Thin TVs have small speakers. A soundbar from about $150 to $250 on HDMI eARC makes dialogue clearer and movies fuller. The LG B5 at around $800 leaves room for one within $1,000; the Samsung S85F and Sony Bravia 5 at about $900 do not. The Hisense U8 and U7 have the best built-in sound in this list.

Prices move around

TV prices on Amazon change often, especially around Black Friday and the Super Bowl. Several picks here sell well below $1,000, so check the current price before you buy. If an OLED drops close to the price of a Mini LED, it is usually the better choice for a dark living room.
