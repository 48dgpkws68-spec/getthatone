Buying guide: how to choose the right tablets for kids

Start with the age the maker gives

Kids tablets are built for an age group. Amazon lists its Fire Kids models for ages 3 to 7 and its Kids Pro and the Kids Tablet 8 in this guide for ages 6 to 12. LeapFrog rates the LeapPad Academy for 3 to 8 and Contixo the V10 for 3 to 7. Apple, Samsung and Lenovo give no age range for their tablets here. The age tells you how thick the case is and what kind of content is included, so it is the best first filter for a gift.

Kids tablet or regular tablet with a case?

A kids tablet comes as a set: case, kids content and parental controls that are on from the start. A regular tablet such as the iPad or the Lenovo Tab One has a wider choice of apps and is useful for longer, but you set up the controls yourself and buy a case on the side. For kids under 6, a ready-made set is usually easier. From about 8, a regular tablet with good controls often makes more sense.

Parental controls: what to look for

Good controls let you do four things: set a daily time limit, set a bedtime, choose which apps and sites are allowed and approve purchases. Amazon does this in its Parent Dashboard, Apple in Screen Time with Family Sharing, Samsung in Samsung Kids and Google in Family Link. All four can be managed from a parent's phone. Set them up before you wrap the tablet, so it is ready to use on Christmas morning.

Screen size and storage

A 7 or 8-inch tablet is easy to hold for kids up to about 7. A 10 or 11-inch screen is better for reading, video and schoolwork, but heavier. For storage, 16GB fills up fast: on the Fire 7 Kids only about 9.5GB is free. With 32GB or more you have room for downloaded shows on a trip. Many models in this guide take a microSD card, which is a cheap way to add space. The iPad does not.

What it costs after you buy it

Look past the price on the box. Amazon includes six months or one year of Kids+, depending on the model, and after that it is a paid monthly subscription that you can cancel. LeapFrog Academy is free for three months after you register. A regular tablet needs a case, and for the iPad the Apple Pencil is sold separately. On the other hand, Amazon's 2-year worry-free guarantee on its Fire Kids tablets can save you the cost of a replacement.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best tablet for kids in 2026?

For most kids from 3 to 7, the Amazon Fire HD 8 Kids is the best pick. The kid-proof case, one year of Amazon Kids+ and a 2-year worry-free guarantee are included. For kids from 6 to 12, the Amazon Kids Tablet 8 with Google Play or the Fire HD 10 Kids Pro fits better.

What age is a kids tablet for?

That differs per model. Amazon lists the Fire 7 Kids, Fire HD 8 Kids and Fire HD 10 Kids for ages 3 to 7 and the Fire HD 10 Kids Pro for ages 6 to 12. LeapFrog rates the LeapPad Academy for ages 3 to 8. Apple, Samsung and Lenovo do not give an age range for the tablets in this guide.

Is an iPad a good tablet for a child?

Yes, if you set it up well. Screen Time lets you set limits per app, block websites and approve purchases through Family Sharing. The iPad comes without a case and without kids content, so add a shockproof case. It is the most expensive option here, but one that a child can keep using for school.

What is the difference between Fire Kids and Fire Kids Pro?

Fire Kids tablets are listed for ages 3 to 7 and come with a thick kid-proof case. Kids Pro tablets are listed for ages 6 to 12 and have a slim case and content for older children. On the Fire HD 10 Kids Pro, kids can request apps and parents approve them.

Do I need Amazon Kids+ to use a Fire Kids tablet?

No. The tablet and the parental controls keep working without it. Kids+ is a library of ad-free books, games, videos and apps. Six months or one year is included, depending on the model. After that it is a paid subscription, which you can cancel in your Amazon account.

When should I order a kids tablet for Christmas?

Black Friday is November 27, 2026 and Cyber Monday November 30. Amazon usually discounts its own kids tablets in that week. Order by mid December to have it before December 25, and charge and set up the tablet before you wrap it.
