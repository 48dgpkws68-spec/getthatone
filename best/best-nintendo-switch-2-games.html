Mario Kart World is made only for the Nintendo Switch 2 and is rated E for Everyone. It is the best-selling game on the system by a wide margin, with more than 15 million copies including console bundles. Races now have 24 drivers, and the courses are linked in one large world. You can drive from one track to the next, and in Free Roam you explore that world at your own pace.

The new Knockout Tour mode is the highlight for many players: a long run across several courses in which the slowest drivers drop out at each checkpoint. Up to 4 people play on one system in split screen, and up to 24 race online. New moves such as rail grinding and wall riding give experienced players something to learn, while steering assists keep young kids on the road. Critics praise the racing and the multiplayer. Free Roam gets mixed reactions, and the price of about $80 is high. As a first Switch 2 game for a household, nothing is a safer choice.

Cons

  • At $79.99 one of the priciest Switch 2 games
  • Free Roam can feel empty after a few hours

Donkey Kong Bananza is a Switch 2 exclusive and is rated E10+ for Everyone 10 and up. It is a 3D platformer built around one idea: nearly everything can be destroyed. Donkey Kong punches through walls, digs tunnels straight down and tears off chunks of ground to throw. The goal is to win back the golden bananas stolen by VoidCo, deep in an underground world of forests, canyons, lagoons and frozen layers.

Pauline rides along, and her singing powers the Bananza transformations, such as the Zebra and Ostrich forms. A second player can join as Pauline in two-player co-op, which works well for a parent with a younger child. Critics rank it among the best games on the system, and it has sold more than 4.7 million copies. It costs about $70, which is $10 less than Mario Kart World for a much longer solo game. That makes it our value pick. Reviewers do mention frame rate dips when a lot is breaking at once. The DK Island and Emerald Rush add-on costs extra.

Kirby Air Riders is a Switch 2 exclusive, rated E10+ by the ESRB for fantasy violence. It is a racing action game with unusually simple controls. Your machine accelerates by itself. One button brakes, drifts and charges a boost, and a second button triggers your rider's special move. Along the way you copy abilities from enemies to attack rivals.

There are several modes. Air Ride is the straight race. City Trial drops up to 16 players online, or 8 locally, into a city to collect power-ups for five minutes before a final contest. Top Ride is a compact top-down race, and Road Trip is the solo mode. Riders include Kirby, Meta Knight and King Dedede. The game sold close to 1.9 million copies in its first months. Fans praise the speed and the amount to unlock. Reviewers note that the screen gets very busy and that it takes a few hours before the systems click. As a second racer next to Mario Kart World, it offers a clearly different feel.

Buying guide: how to choose the right Nintendo Switch 2 games

Switch 2 game or Nintendo Switch 2 Edition?

Some games are made only for the Switch 2, such as Mario Kart World, Donkey Kong Bananza and Kirby Air Riders. A Nintendo Switch 2 Edition is an upgraded version of a game that also exists for the older Switch, with better visuals and sometimes extra content. If you already own the Switch version, look for the cheaper upgrade pack first. Most older Switch games also run on the Switch 2.

Check the ESRB rating

Every box shows a rating from the ESRB. E means Everyone. E10+ means Everyone 10 and up. T means Teen, for ages 13 and up. M means Mature, for ages 17 and up. In this list, four games are rated E, four E10+, one T and one M. The rating is on the front of the box and on the Amazon page. The Switch 2 has parental controls to block games by rating.

How many people will play?

For a family, look at the number of players on one system. Mario Kart World, Super Mario Party Jamboree and Mario Tennis Fever take up to 4 players on one screen. Donkey Kong Bananza and Kirby and the Forgotten Land have two-player co-op. Metroid Prime 4 and Cyberpunk 2077 are for one player. Extra players need extra controllers: a Switch 2 comes with one pair of Joy-Con 2.

Physical box or download

A boxed game can be wrapped, lent to a friend and resold. A download is on the console right away and cannot get lost. Note that some boxes hold a game-key card: the card works as a key and the game itself still has to be downloaded. That is stated on the front of the box. Online play in most games requires a paid Nintendo Switch Online membership.

What do Switch 2 games cost?

Most big Switch 2 games have a list price of $69.99, and a few cost $79.99. Nintendo games rarely get deep discounts, but Amazon does sometimes drop $5 to $15 off, mostly around Black Friday on November 27, 2026. Extra story content is often sold separately, so check what is in the box. Prices here are indications.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best Nintendo Switch 2 game in 2026?

For most households, Mario Kart World is the best pick. It is rated E, easy for beginners and supports up to 4 players on one system and 24 online. For a solo player, Donkey Kong Bananza is the stronger choice, with a long 3D adventure and two-player co-op.

Which Switch 2 games are good for young kids?

Mario Kart World, Super Mario Party Jamboree, Pokémon Pokopia and Mario Tennis Fever are all rated E for Everyone. Kirby and the Forgotten Land is rated E10+ but has a gentle difficulty and two-player co-op, which makes it a good first 3D game with a parent.

Do Nintendo Switch games work on the Switch 2?

Most Nintendo Switch games run on the Switch 2. For some of them, Nintendo sells a Nintendo Switch 2 Edition with better visuals or extra content. If you already own the Switch game, you can usually buy an upgrade pack for less than a new copy.

What does Nintendo Switch 2 Edition mean?

It is an enhanced version of a game that first came out for the Nintendo Switch. It only runs on the Switch 2. Examples in this list are Pokémon Legends: Z-A, Super Mario Party Jamboree, Metroid Prime 4: Beyond and Kirby and the Forgotten Land.

What is a game-key card?

A game-key card is a card in a normal game box that does not hold the full game. You insert it, download the game and keep the card in the console to play. It can still be lent or resold. The box states clearly when it contains a game-key card.

What is the best Switch 2 game for two players?

For playing against each other, Mario Kart World and Mario Tennis Fever are the best picks. For playing together, Donkey Kong Bananza and Kirby and the Forgotten Land have two-player co-op on one system. Super Mario Party Jamboree works with two but is more fun with three or four.
