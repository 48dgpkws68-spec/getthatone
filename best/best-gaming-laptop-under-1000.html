Buying guide: how to choose the right gaming laptop under 1000

What $1,000 buys in late 2026

Less than a year ago. Memory and storage became much more expensive in 2026 and laptop makers passed that on. Under $1,000 you now mostly find three graphics chips: the old RTX 3050 from about $700, the RTX 4050 from about $870 and, right at the limit, the current RTX 5050. An RTX 5060 starts at about $1,050, just outside this budget.

RTX 3050, RTX 4050 or RTX 5050?

The graphics chip decides your frame rate. The RTX 3050 is fine for Fortnite, Valorant and Minecraft, but new story games need low settings. The RTX 4050 with 6GB of video memory is the sweet spot of this budget and runs most games well at 1080p with DLSS. The RTX 5050 is faster again, has 8GB of video memory and supports DLSS 4, so it will stay usable the longest.

Insist on 16GB of memory

16GB of memory is the minimum for new games. Some laptops under $1,000 still ship with 8GB, such as the ASUS TUF Gaming A15 in this list. That is only acceptable if the laptop has a free memory slot and you add a module yourself. Storage is 512GB on almost every model here, which holds four or five big games. Check whether there is a second SSD slot if you play many titles.

The screen is where makers save

Every laptop in this list has a full HD class screen at 144Hz, which matches these graphics chips well. The savings are in brightness and color. Tom's Hardware calls the 250 nit panel of the MSI Katana 15 HX dim and washed out. The Lenovo LOQ 15 lists 300 nits, which is easier to use in a bright room. If you also edit photos, connect an external monitor.

Watch the Amazon listing

Many gaming laptops on Amazon are sold by resellers who add memory, storage or accessories and change the Windows version. Those bundles often cost more than $1,000 while the title looks almost the same. Check that the listing names the exact graphics chip, memory and storage of the model in this guide, and look at who the seller is and how the warranty works.

Prices and Black Friday

Black Friday is November 27, 2026 and Cyber Monday is November 30. Laptops under $1,000 are often on sale this week, but prices also moved up during the year. The prices in this guide are reference prices from US stores and price comparison sites in November 2026 and change daily, so compare your pick at two or three stores before you order.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best gaming laptop under $1,000?

The MSI Katana 15 HX B14WEK. It has an RTX 5050 with 8GB of video memory, a 16 core Intel Core i7-14650HX, 16GB of DDR5 and a 1TB SSD for $999.99. Tom's Hardware calls it the rare gaming laptop it has reviewed under $1,000, with a dim screen as the main weakness.

Can you get an RTX 5060 laptop under $1,000?

Not at regular prices in November 2026. The cheapest RTX 5060 laptop in our main guide, the Acer Nitro V 17, costs about $1,050. Under $1,000 the RTX 5050 is the fastest current chip.

Is an RTX 4050 still good enough in 2026?

Yes, for 1080p. Popular online games run above 100 frames per second and most story games work at medium to high settings with DLSS switched on. Its 6GB of video memory limits the highest texture settings in new games.

Is 8GB of memory enough for a gaming laptop?

No. New games and Windows together need 16GB. If a laptop ships with 8GB, check that it has a free memory slot and add a second module, or choose a model with 16GB from the start.

What is the cheapest gaming laptop worth buying?

The MSI Thin A15 B7UC-473US at about $700. It has an RTX 3050, a Ryzen 5 7535HS and 16GB of DDR5. It is fine for games like Fortnite and Valorant, but too slow for new story games at high settings.

Should I wait or buy a gaming laptop under $1,000 now?

Laptop prices rose during 2026 because memory became more expensive, so waiting is not sure to help. If you find one of the RTX 4050 or RTX 5050 laptops in this list at or below our reference price during Black Friday week, that is a fair moment to buy.
