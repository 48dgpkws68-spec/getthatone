Buying guide: how to choose the right infrared space heaters

What does an infrared heater actually do?

Infrared heaters use quartz tubes, carbon elements or mica panels that give off radiant heat. That heat warms people, furniture and walls in its path, much like sunshine, so you feel warm quickly even when the air is still cool. Many US models add a fan that blows air over the hot elements, which spreads warmth through the room as well.

Do infrared heaters save money?

Every 1,500-watt electric heater turns almost all its power into heat, so infrared is not more efficient per watt. It can save money because you feel warm faster and can heat just the spot where you sit, which lets you lower the central thermostat. At about 17 cents per kWh, an hour on high costs around 25 cents.

Quartz tower, cabinet or wall unit?

Quartz towers are cheap and give fast, direct warmth at a desk or chair. Cabinet heaters with a fan heat a whole room more evenly and often have a digital thermostat and remote. Wall-mounted infrared heaters save floor space in small apartments, bathrooms and offices, but take more effort to install.

How much heater do you need?

Plan on about 10 watts per square foot, so a 1,500-watt heater suits roughly 150 square feet as the only heat source and larger rooms as a supplement. Manufacturer claims of 1,000 square feet are optimistic. In a big, poorly insulated room, a cabinet heater with a fan works better than a pure radiant tower.

Thermostat and eco mode

A digital thermostat or eco mode makes the biggest difference to running costs because it cycles the heater once the room is warm. Cheaper towers with only high and low settings keep running at full power. A remote and timer are useful extras for bedrooms and living rooms.

Safety and placement

Choose a heater with tip-over and overheat shutoff and a UL or ETL listing. Plug it directly into a wall outlet, never an extension cord, and keep three feet of clearance from curtains, bedding and furniture. Point radiant heaters toward where you sit, not at a wall, and keep glowing elements away from children and pets.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best infrared space heater in 2026?

Based on our comparison of specs, prices and buyer reviews, the Dr Infrared Heater DR-968 is the best infrared space heater for most homes thanks to strong output and a digital thermostat. The Comfort Zone CZQTV007BK is the best value for direct warmth.

Are infrared heaters better than oil filled heaters?

They suit different needs. Infrared heaters warm you within seconds and work well in drafty rooms or for heating one spot. Oil-filled radiators are slower but silent and better at keeping a closed bedroom at a steady temperature.

Is an infrared heater safe to leave on?

Modern infrared heaters with tip-over and overheat protection and a UL or ETL listing are safe to run while you are home. Cabinet models keep the hot elements behind a grille. Do not run any space heater while you sleep unless it has a thermostat and is placed well away from bedding.

Do infrared heaters dry out the air?

Radiant heat does not remove moisture, but any heating raises the temperature and lowers relative humidity, so winter air can still feel dry. If that bothers you, a heater with a built-in humidifier such as the Dr Infrared DR-998 helps.

Can an infrared heater heat a whole room?

A cabinet heater with a fan, such as the Dr Infrared DR-968, can heat a large room. Quartz towers without much airflow mainly warm what is in front of them, so they are best for a desk, reading chair or workshop bench.

Are there infrared panels in the US like in Europe?

Wall-mounted infrared panels are less common in the US than in Europe, where they are often used as main heating. The closest plug-in options on Amazon are slim units like the Heat Storm Phoenix, which can hang on a wall or stand on feet.

Is infrared heat safe for your skin and eyes?

Infrared from a space heater is simply heat, the same kind you feel from a fire or a warm stove. It is not the same as the UV light in tanning lamps. Sitting very close for hours can dry or redden skin, as with a fireplace, so keep a few feet of distance and do not stare into glowing quartz tubes. Cabinet heaters like the Dr Infrared DR-968 keep the elements behind a grille.

Can you use an infrared heater in a garage?

Only if the manual allows it and the garage is used as a living or work space, not as storage for fuel. Keep the heater well away from gasoline, paint, solvents, sawdust and other flammable materials, and never leave it running unattended. A radiant tower like the Comfort Zone CZQTV007BK warms you directly at a workbench, while a cabinet heater with a fan is better at warming the whole space.
