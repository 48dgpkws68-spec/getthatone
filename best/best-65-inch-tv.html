Buying guide: how to choose the right 65-inch TVs

OLED, QD-OLED or Mini LED?

OLED and QD-OLED pixels light themselves, so blacks are perfect and contrast is unbeatable. That is ideal for movies in a dim room. QD-OLED adds brighter, more saturated color. Mini LED uses thousands of small backlight zones and gets much brighter, which helps in a sunny living room, though you may see halos around bright objects.

Is your room big enough for 65 inches?

For a 65-inch 4K TV, a viewing distance of about 7 to 9 feet works best. A 65-inch TV is roughly 57 inches wide and 33 inches tall without the stand, so measure your wall or TV stand first. Check where the feet go: some models put them at the far edges.

Which connections do you need?

Count your devices. A PS5, Xbox Series X or gaming PC benefits from HDMI 2.1 with 120Hz or more. A soundbar needs the HDMI eARC port. TVs like the LG C5, Samsung S90F and Samsung QN90F have four HDMI 2.1 ports, while many Mini LED and Sony models have two.

Dolby Vision or HDR10+?

Netflix, Disney+ and Apple TV+ mostly use Dolby Vision, while Prime Video also uses HDR10+. LG and Sony support Dolby Vision only, Samsung supports HDR10+ only, and TCL and Hisense support both. Brightness matters more than the format: a bright TV gets far more out of HDR.

How much brightness do you need?

In a dark room in the evening, any OLED or good Mini LED is bright enough. If your TV faces windows or you watch a lot of daytime sports, pick a very bright Mini LED like the Hisense U8 or TCL QM7K, or a bright OLED like the LG G5. A matte screen like the Samsung QN90F helps against reflections.

Is OLED burn-in still a problem?

With normal, varied viewing, burn-in is rare on modern OLEDs. They shift pixels slightly and run refresh cycles when the TV is off. The risk is higher if you leave a news channel with a static logo or a game HUD on screen for many hours every day. In that case, a Mini LED is the worry-free choice.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best 65-inch TV in 2026?

Based on our comparison of specs, prices and buyer reviews, the LG C5 OLED65C5PUA is the best 65-inch TV for most people. It combines OLED contrast, four HDMI 2.1 ports at up to 144Hz and Dolby Vision. The TCL QM7K 65QM7K is the best value.

How much should I spend on a 65-inch TV?

A good 65-inch TV costs between about $500 and $1,400 in 2026. Around $500 buys a Mini LED like the TCL QM6K, $700 to $800 a very bright Mini LED like the TCL QM7K or Hisense U8, and OLEDs start around $1,000.

How far should I sit from a 65-inch TV?

About 7 to 9 feet is ideal for a 65-inch 4K TV. Closer than 6 feet can feel overwhelming; farther than 10 feet and you might want a 75-inch model.

Is OLED or Mini LED better at 65 inches?

OLED is better for movies in a dim room thanks to perfect blacks and wide viewing angles. Mini LED is better in bright rooms and costs less for the same size. Many buyers choose OLED for a home theater and Mini LED for a sunny family room.

Which 65-inch TV is best for gaming?

The LG C5 and LG G5 are the top gaming TVs, with four HDMI 2.1 ports and up to 144Hz or 165Hz. The Samsung S90F is just as fast. On a budget, the TCL QM7K and Hisense U8 offer 144Hz to 165Hz for far less.

How much does a 65-inch TV weigh?

Most 65-inch TVs weigh between about 45 and 70 pounds with the stand. Mini LED models are usually heavier than OLEDs. Use a wall mount rated for the TV's weight and VESA pattern.
