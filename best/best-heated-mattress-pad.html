Buying guide: how to choose the right heated mattress pads

Heated mattress pad or heated blanket?

A heated mattress pad sits under your fitted sheet and warms you from below. Because heat rises, it feels warmer at a lower setting, and you can keep your own comforter on top. A heated blanket warms from above and is easier to move to the couch. Many people who sleep cold use a pad in bed and a heated throw in the living room.

Get dual controls for a shared bed

Queen, king and California king pads usually come with two controllers, one for each side. That matters because two people rarely want the same warmth. Twin and full sizes usually have one controller. If one sleeper has cold feet, consider a multi-zone pad with separate body and foot settings.

Check the pocket depth

Measure your mattress height, including any topper, before you buy. Pads list a maximum depth, often 15, 16 or 18 inches. If the pocket is too shallow, the corners pop off and the heating wires can bunch up, which is uncomfortable and not good for the pad.

Timer and safety features

Look for an auto shut-off you can set, ideally from 1 to 12 hours, plus overheat protection and an ETL or UL listing. Preheat warms the bed quickly before you get in, and memory keeps your last setting. Never fold a heated pad while it is on, and keep the controllers uncovered.

Fabric and feel

Quilted polyester is the most common top and adds a bit of padding. Cotton breathes better and suits people who run warm. Fleece and sherpa tops feel cozy but can be too warm outside winter. A waterproof pad saves you a separate mattress protector.

Running cost and care

A queen heated pad typically uses around 60 to 100 watts per side on high and much less on low. At average US electricity prices that is only a few cents per night, often less than turning up the thermostat. Detach the controllers and follow the label: most pads can be machine washed on gentle and tumble dried on low.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best heated mattress pad in 2026?

Based on our comparison of specs, prices and buyer reviews, the Sunbeam Restful Quilted Heated Mattress Pad is the best for most people. It has 12 heat settings per side, dual controllers, a 1 to 12 hour timer and is fully washable. The Bedsure Heated Mattress Pad is the best value.

Is it safe to sleep on a heated mattress pad all night?

Modern pads with overheat protection, an auto shut-off timer and an ETL or UL listing are designed for overnight use. Use a low setting, never fold or tuck the pad while it is on, keep the controllers uncovered and replace any pad with damaged wires. Babies, people with diabetes or reduced sensation and anyone who is pregnant should check with a doctor first.

Can I put a heated mattress pad on a memory foam mattress?

Most manufacturers allow it, but check the pad's manual. Memory foam softens with heat, so a low setting is usually enough. Do not use a heated pad together with an electric blanket at the same time.

Does a heated mattress pad go under or over the sheet?

It goes on top of the mattress and under the fitted sheet. If you use a mattress protector, put the protector on the mattress first, then the heated pad, then the sheet, unless the pad's manual says otherwise.

How much electricity does a heated mattress pad use?

A queen pad on high typically draws around 60 to 100 watts per side and far less on low settings. Over an eight hour night that usually costs a few cents, which is cheaper than heating the whole bedroom.

Can you wash a heated mattress pad?

Yes, most can go in the washing machine once the controllers are unplugged. Use a gentle cycle with cold or warm water and tumble dry on low, or follow the care label. Make sure the pad and connectors are completely dry before using it again.

How long does a heated mattress pad take to warm up?

It depends on the setting. On low, a pad like the Sunbeam Restful can need 20 to 30 minutes before the bed feels warm. Start on a high level, then turn it down once you get in. Pads with a preheat mode, such as the Bedsure models, run at full power for a short time so the bed is warm within minutes.

Can you use a heated mattress pad and an electric blanket together?

No. Do not run a heated mattress pad and an electric blanket at the same time. Trapping heat from both sides can overheat the bed. Choose one. A pad suits people who want heat from below and want to keep their own comforter. A blanket suits people who also want to use it on the couch.
