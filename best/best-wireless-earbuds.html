Buying guide: how to choose the right wireless earbuds

Match your earbuds to your phone

AirPods pair instantly with iPhones and unlock features like automatic switching, heart rate tracking and hearing tools. Galaxy Buds work best with Samsung phones and Pixel Buds with Pixel and recent Android phones. Sony, Bose, Technics, Beats and Soundcore work equally well on both platforms through their own apps.

How good is noise cancellation?

Active noise cancellation reduces constant low sounds like engines, air conditioning and traffic. Bose and Sony lead, followed closely by AirPods Pro 3. Budget models with ANC take the edge off but do not create silence. A good seal from the right ear tip size is just as important as the technology.

Battery life

Most earbuds last 6 to 10 hours per charge with noise cancellation on, and the case adds two to four more charges. For long flights, choose a model with at least 8 hours per charge. Wireless charging cases are convenient if you already have a charging pad.

Fit and comfort

Try every size of ear tip that comes in the box; a poor seal ruins both bass and noise cancellation. For workouts, look for wing tips or ear hooks like the Powerbeats Pro 2. If you dislike silicone tips, open-fit models like AirPods 4 are more comfortable but block less noise.

Codecs and sound quality

iPhones use AAC, so high-resolution codecs make no difference there. On Android, LDAC (Sony, Technics) and aptX Adaptive (Bose with Snapdragon phones) can stream more detail. In practice, fit, tuning and noise cancellation matter more to sound quality than the codec.

Calls and multipoint

If you take many calls, check reviews for microphone quality in noisy places. Multipoint lets earbuds connect to two devices at once, such as a phone and laptop, and switch automatically. Technics supports three devices, which is ideal for remote workers.

Frequently asked questions

What are the best wireless earbuds in 2026?

Based on our comparison of specs, prices and buyer reviews, the Apple AirPods Pro 3 are the best wireless earbuds for most people, especially iPhone users. The Soundcore P40i are the best value, and the Sony WF-1000XM6 are the best premium pick for any phone.

Which earbuds have the best noise cancellation?

The Bose QuietComfort Ultra Earbuds (2nd Gen) and Sony WF-1000XM6 have the strongest noise cancellation, followed closely by the AirPods Pro 3. They work well on airplanes and busy commutes.

What are the best earbuds for Android?

Pixel owners should choose the Pixel Buds Pro 2 and Samsung owners the Galaxy Buds4 Pro. For any Android phone, the Sony WF-1000XM6 and Technics EAH-AZ100 offer high-resolution LDAC audio.

Are cheap wireless earbuds worth it?

Yes, for casual listening, workouts and kids. The Soundcore P40i offer noise cancellation and long battery life for about $50, and the JLab Go Air Pop work well as a backup pair. Premium models offer better sound, calls and noise cancellation.

What are the best earbuds for working out?

The Beats Powerbeats Pro 2 are the best for workouts thanks to ear hooks, heart rate sensing and an IPX4 rating. The AirPods Pro 3 are also excellent, with IP57 water resistance and built-in heart rate tracking.

How long do wireless earbuds last?

Most earbuds last two to four years before battery capacity drops noticeably. Store them in the case, keep them clean and avoid extreme heat to help batteries last longer.
