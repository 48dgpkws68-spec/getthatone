Buying guide: how to choose the right 43 inch TV under 300

Is 43 inches the right size?

A 43-inch TV suits a viewing distance of roughly 4 to 6 feet, which is typical for bedrooms, kitchens and dorm rooms. For a living room couch 7 feet or more away, a 50 or 55-inch screen looks better, and our guide to the best TVs under $300 shows those cost little more. At 43 inches, 4K still makes text and streaming look sharper than 1080p when you sit close.

QLED, Mini LED or LED?

Under $300 most 43-inch TVs are basic LED. QLED models like the TCL F7D and Hisense E6 add a quantum dot layer for richer color, often for only $20 to $50 more. The Samsung M70H is the rare 43-inch Mini LED, with better brightness control. None of these small sets has many dimming zones, so do not expect deep blacks in a dark room.

HDR formats

Netflix and Disney+ mostly stream in Dolby Vision, and Prime Video also uses HDR10+. The TCL F7D, Hisense E6 and Hisense A7 support both. The Hisense E6SR has Dolby Vision but not HDR10+, Samsung has HDR10+ only, and the LG UA77, Roku, Amazon 4-Series and Insignia stop at HDR10. Small budget TVs are not very bright, so HDR formats matter less than at bigger sizes.

Fire TV, Roku, Tizen or webOS?

All four have the major US apps. Fire TV works best with Alexa and Prime Video but shows more promotions. Roku OS is the simplest to learn and a good choice for kids or older relatives. Samsung Tizen and LG webOS are quick and include free live channels. AirPlay is built into the TCL, Hisense, LG and Insignia models here.

Gaming on a small TV

Every TV in this list has a native 60Hz panel, so a PS5 or Xbox Series X runs at up to 60 frames per second. Look for ALLM or a game mode to cut input lag; the Hisense E6SR and LG UA77 also support VRR. A Motion Rate of 120 or 240 on the box is processing, not a faster screen. For 120Hz gaming you need a bigger, pricier TV.

Prices move around

Budget TV prices on Amazon change often, especially around Black Friday. The Samsung M70H only fits this guide on sale, while the TCL F7D and Hisense E6 regularly drop well below $300. Check the current price and the exact model number before you buy, and skip renewed listings unless you want one on purpose.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best 43-inch TV under $300?

The TCL F7D. It is a 2026 QLED with Dolby Vision, HDR10+, Dolby Atmos and AirPlay 2 on Fire TV, and it usually sells for about $250 to $300.

What is the cheapest good 43-inch 4K TV?

The Hisense E6 Cinema Series offers QLED color and Dolby Vision for about $200. If you only need the basics, the Insignia F50 costs around $150.

Is there a 43-inch Mini LED TV under $300?

Yes, the Samsung M70H, but only when it is on sale for about $280. It supports HDR10+ but not Dolby Vision.

Which 43-inch TV under $300 is best for gaming?

None has a 120Hz panel, so all run consoles at up to 60fps. The Hisense E6SR and LG UA77 add VRR, and the TCL F7D and Hisense E6 have auto low latency modes.

Should I buy a 43-inch or a 50-inch TV for $300?

If you sit more than 6 feet away, get 50 or 55 inches; $300 buys a good one. For a bedroom, kitchen or desk, 43 inches is easier to place and still sharp in 4K.
