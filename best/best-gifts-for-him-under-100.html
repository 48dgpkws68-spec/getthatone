Buying guide: how to choose the right gifts for him under $100

Spend more on what he uses daily

With up to $100 you can afford the better version of something he already uses: earbuds for his commute, a mouse for his workday or a shaver for every morning. A daily upgrade feels more valuable than a gadget for a hobby he does not have.

Stick with known brands

Brands like Soundcore, Leatherman, Braun, Logitech and Weber come with a real warranty and spare parts. Leatherman gives 25 years, Bellroy 3 years. Cheap copies with piles of suspect reviews often break within a year.

Check compatibility first

The Xbox controller works with Xbox, PC and phones, but not with PlayStation or Switch. The Amazfit Bip 6 works with iPhone and Android. A quick look at his devices avoids a gift he cannot use.

Think about running costs

A Braun shaver needs a new head about every 18 months, the Xbox controller uses AA batteries and the Weber grill needs charcoal. Adding a bag of charcoal or a rechargeable battery pack makes the gift complete.

Black Friday and Christmas

Black Friday falls on November 27, 2026. Earbuds, shavers and massage guns often get the biggest discounts, while LEGO sets and Leatherman tools can sell out in December. Order early and use a gift receipt in case he wants a different color.

Make it personal

Some Leatherman tools can be engraved, and a small extra goes a long way: a bag of charcoal with the grill, a mouse pad with the MX Master or a game with the controller. Amazon gift wrap makes it easy to ship straight to him.
