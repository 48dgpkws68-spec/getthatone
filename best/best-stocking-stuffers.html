Most cost less than $20. For every toy and game we list the age the maker gives, so you know which stocking it belongs in.

UNO from Mattel Games is listed for ages 7 and up and for 2 to 10 players, and it is hard to find a stocking stuffer with a wider reach. Players match the top card of the pile by color or number and try to get rid of their hand first. Skip, Reverse, Draw Two and Wild Draw Four cards turn a quiet round into a noisy one, and whoever forgets to shout UNO with one card left draws two more.

The deck has 25 cards in each of the four colors plus the wild cards. Depending on the edition, Amazon listings mention 108 or 112 cards, since newer decks add a few special wild cards. A round takes minutes and needs nothing but a table, which is why buyers take it on planes, to restaurants and on camping trips. Since many homes already have a worn deck, a fresh one is still welcome. Mattel also sells versions like UNO Flip and UNO Show 'em No Mercy for players who know the classic too well.

The Rubik's Cube is rated for ages 8 and up, and it is the kind of small gift that outlasts everything else in the stocking. The current official 3x3 from Spin Master has colored tiles instead of stickers, so nothing peels off, and Amazon listings describe smoother turning than the cubes parents remember from their own childhood.

Almost nobody solves it without help the first time. Rubik's offers free step by step guides and videos, and the layer by layer method consists of short move sequences that a child of 8 can memorize. Buyers say the first full solve often takes a few days of practice. After that, kids start timing themselves and racing friends, and many adults pick it up again too. It needs no batteries and survives being dropped. For a child who gets stuck easily, there is also a Coach version with learning stickers. Check that you order the standard 3x3 and not a keychain or mini version, which Amazon shows next to it.

The second generation AirTag went on sale in early 2026 and is the most useful small gift for an iPhone owner. Attach it to keys, slip it into a wallet or a suitcase and it shows up in the Find My app. Because it uses Apple's network of devices, it can also be located far from home, for example when luggage does not arrive at the airport.

Compared with the first AirTag, Apple states that Precision Finding works from up to 1.5 times as far on a recent iPhone and that the built-in speaker is about 50 percent louder. It is rated IP67 against water and dust, and a standard CR2032 coin cell lasts more than a year and is easy to replace. The catch is important: it only works with Apple devices, so it is no gift for an Android user. It also has no hole, so a key ring or holder costs extra. An AirTag is not a toy and is not meant for tracking people.

Taco Cat Goat Cheese Pizza is a slapping card game that has become a staple under the tree. Players take turns placing a card face up while saying the next word in the sequence: taco, cat, goat, cheese, pizza. When the card matches the word that is spoken, everyone slaps the pile, and the last hand on top takes all the cards. Whoever gets rid of their cards first wins.

Special cards like the Gorilla, Groundhog and Narwhal require a silly gesture before you may slap, and that is where the laughing starts. The game is listed for 2 to 8 players from age 7, and a round takes 10 to 15 minutes. Buyers say it works across generations and that kids often beat adults, because they are quicker. It does get loud, and with enthusiastic players hands get hit, so rings and watches are better taken off. The box is about the size of a deck of cards. There are several sequel versions with other words, which can be mixed in.

And it can tear if it is pulled hard or bitten, so it is not for children who still put things in their mouths. A lip balm is the stocking stuffer that actually gets used up, and Burt's Bees Beeswax is the best known one in the US. The Amazon listing for this pack contains four tubes of 0.15 ounce each. The balm is made with beeswax, vitamin E and a touch of peppermint oil, which gives a light cooling tingle. It has no tint, so it works for everybody in the house.

A four pack is practical in December: one tube per stocking, or one each for the coat pocket, the car, the desk and the nightstand. Buyers have used the same balm for years and mainly praise that it is not greasy. The peppermint is a matter of taste, and people with sensitive lips sometimes prefer an unscented balm. It contains no sun protection, so it is not the right pick for a ski trip. Amazon lists several four packs that look alike, including fruit flavor mixes, so check that the listing says Beeswax if you want the original.

The Original Slinky is the cheapest gift in this list and probably the one that needs the least explanation. The maker recommends it for ages 5 and up. It is a metal spring with a diameter of 2.75 inches that walks down a staircase step by step once you give it a push, and that stretches and flips from hand to hand.

According to the Amazon listing, mechanical engineer Richard James invented it in 1943 and it went on sale at Gimbels department store two years later. More than 300 million have been sold. Buyers like that it is still made of metal and that it works without batteries. The known weakness has not changed in eighty years: once the coils are tangled or bent, a Slinky rarely recovers, so it is not a toy to pull apart with two kids. It also walks best on stairs with steps that are not too deep. Amazon sells single springs and packs of three, plus a plastic version, so check which one is in your cart.

Buying guide: how to choose the right stocking stuffers

Check that it fits

A standard Christmas stocking is narrow. Everything in this list is small: a deck of cards, a 2.25 inch cube, a rolled up beanie or a bag of truffles. Boxed games larger than a deck of cards and anything rigid over about 6 inches wide usually do not fit.

Mix something fun with something useful

A good stocking has a toy or game, a treat and one thing that gets used every day. For example UNO, Lindt truffles and a lip balm. Useful gifts like a charger or a beanie are the ones adults and teens appreciate most a month later.

Mind the maker age for kids

We list the age the maker gives. The NeeDoh Nice Cube is rated 3 and up, the Slinky 5 and up, UNO and Taco Cat Goat Cheese Pizza 7 and up and the Rubik's Cube 8 and up. Small toys and coin cell batteries, like the one in an AirTag, do not belong near toddlers.

Set a budget per stocking

Small gifts add up fast when you fill four stockings. Most items here cost $5 to $12. One larger item of $15 to $30, such as a beanie, a charger or an AirTag, with two or three small ones keeps a stocking around $40 to $50.

Split multi packs

Multi packs lower the price per stocking. The Burt's Bees pack has four tubes and a bag of LINDOR truffles can be divided over the whole family. Slinky is also sold in packs of three. Check the price per piece, because single items are sometimes cheaper.

Order on time

Black Friday is November 27, 2026 and Cyber Monday November 30. Small popular items can sell out in the last two weeks before Christmas, and colors of beanies and squeeze toys go first. Order by mid December for delivery before December 25. Prices here are guide prices.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best stocking stuffer?

For most families UNO is the best choice. It costs about $6, is listed for ages 7 and up and 2 to 10 players, and fits in any stocking. On a tighter budget, the Original Slinky costs about $5.

What are good stocking stuffers for kids?

Popular picks are the NeeDoh Nice Cube (maker age 3 and up), the Original Slinky (5 and up), UNO and Taco Cat Goat Cheese Pizza (7 and up) and the Rubik's Cube (8 and up). A bag of chocolate truffles fills up the rest.

What are good stocking stuffers for adults?

Useful small gifts work best: Burt's Bees lip balm, a Carhartt Knit Cuffed Beanie, an Anker Nano 30W charger or an Apple AirTag for an iPhone user. Lindt LINDOR truffles are a safe treat.

What are good stocking stuffers for teens?

Teens like things they would pick themselves: a Carhartt beanie, a NeeDoh Nice Cube, a fast phone charger, a Rubik's Cube or an AirTag for a backpack or keys if they have an iPhone.

How much should I spend on stocking stuffers?

Many families spend $25 to $50 per stocking. With items of $5 to $12 and one gift of $15 to $30 you fill a stocking in that range. There is no rule, and a few well chosen small gifts are enough.

What do you put in a stocking besides candy?

A card game, a small puzzle or fidget toy, a lip balm, warm accessories such as a beanie, and a small piece of tech like a charger or a tracker. Aim for things that get used after the holidays.
