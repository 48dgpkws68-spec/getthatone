Buying guide: how to choose the right TVs under $500

What does $500 buy in 2026?

For $500 or less you can get a 55-inch Mini LED TV with 144Hz gaming, Dolby Vision and HDR10+, or a 65-inch Mini LED if you shop deals. Cheaper sets around $250 to $350 are usually QLED or basic LED with a 60Hz panel. They are fine for streaming but look flatter in HDR.

Mini LED vs QLED vs LED

Mini LED uses hundreds of tiny backlight zones that dim and brighten independently, which gives much better contrast and brightness. QLED adds a quantum dot layer for richer color but often has no local dimming. Plain LED is the cheapest. In this budget, Mini LED is the biggest upgrade you can buy.

Big brand or budget brand?

TCL and Hisense give you more brightness and gaming features per dollar. Samsung and LG cost a bit more for the same specs but offer polished software and familiar menus. Roku TVs are the easiest to use. Check the warranty too: Hisense gives two years on the U6, most others one year.

Pick the right smart platform

Google TV (TCL, Hisense U7) has the largest app selection and built-in Chromecast. Fire TV (Hisense U6) is best for Alexa homes but shows more ads. Roku OS is the simplest. Samsung Tizen and LG webOS are fast and include free channels. Any TV can be upgraded later with a cheap streaming stick.

Refresh rate matters for gaming

A native 120Hz or 144Hz panel with VRR makes PS5, Xbox Series X and PC games look smoother and feel more responsive. Many cheap TVs advertise a Motion Rate or 120Hz with DLG, which only works at lower resolution. Look for the word native in the specs if you game a lot.

Budget for sound

Thin TVs have small speakers, so dialogue can sound thin. A soundbar connected via HDMI eARC is the easiest upgrade, and a good one starts around $150. If you are spending your full $500 on the TV, choose a model with better built-in audio, like the TCL QM7K or Hisense U7.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best TV under $500 in 2026?

Based on our comparison of specs, prices and buyer reviews, the TCL QM6K 55QM6K is the best TV under $500 for most people. It offers Mini LED brightness, native 144Hz, Dolby Vision and HDR10+. The Hisense U6 55U65QF is the best value at around $300.

Can I get a 65-inch TV for under $500?

Yes. The 65-inch TCL QM6K is regularly priced around $500 and has Mini LED and 144Hz. The 65-inch Hisense U6 often costs less. Basic 65-inch LED TVs cost even less, but their contrast and HDR are much weaker.

Is a Mini LED TV worth it on a budget?

Yes. Mini LED gives far better contrast and brightness than a regular LED TV, and in 2026 it starts around $300. It is the most noticeable picture upgrade you can buy for under $500.

Which TV under $500 is best for gaming?

The Hisense U7 55U75QG with native 165Hz and VRR is the fastest, followed by the TCL QM6K and QM7K at 144Hz. Avoid 60Hz models like the Roku Select Series and LG UA77 if you want 120fps on a PS5 or Xbox Series X.

When are TVs cheapest?

Prices drop most around Black Friday in late November and before the Super Bowl in late January and early February. Previous-year models also get cheaper in spring when new lineups arrive.

Do cheap TVs last as long as expensive ones?

Most modern LED and Mini LED TVs last many years with normal use. Budget brands like TCL and Hisense are generally reliable. Plug the TV into a surge protector and check the warranty length before you buy.
