The 10 Best Gifts for 10 Year Olds of 2026

A 10 year old is too old for most toys and not yet a teenager. Kids this age want gifts that feel real: a strategy game adults also play, a robot to code, a proper chemistry set or a LEGO Technic model with over a thousand pieces. Every gift in this list has a maker age rating that includes 10 year olds, and most are rated 10 and up. We compared ten popular gifts for 10 year olds on Amazon, from CATAN and Anomia to the Sphero BOLT+ and the LEGO Technic Mars Rover, on price, specs and buyer reviews.

CATAN is rated by the maker for ages 10 and up, and 10 is the age at which many kids move from children's games to this one. Players collect wood, brick, wool, grain and ore, trade them with each other and build roads, settlements and cities on an island of hex tiles. The first player to reach 10 points wins. Because the tiles are laid out differently each time, no two games are the same.

The current 6th Edition has new artwork and a reworked rulebook, and is for 3 to 4 players with a playtime of 60 to 90 minutes. Buyers say a 10 year old learns the rules in one game and is soon bargaining harder than the adults. The trading is the heart of the game and also the reason for the occasional argument. A separate extension for 5 to 6 players is also rated 10+. The Seafarers and Cities and Knights expansions are rated 12+, so save those for later.

Anomia is rated for ages 10 and up and is for 3 to 6 players. Each player flips a card with a symbol and a category, such as a dog breed, a candy bar or a river. Nothing happens until the symbols on two players' cards match. Then those two race to shout an example of the category on the other person's card. The faster player wins the card, which can uncover a new match and start a chain reaction.

It sounds simple, and it is, but under time pressure even easy answers disappear from your head. That is the joke of the game, and it is why it works so well with a mixed group of kids and adults. A round takes about 30 minutes. Buyers describe a lot of laughing and shouting, so it is not a quiet evening game. At about $17 it is the cheapest gift in this list. Other editions exist: the Party Edition is also 10+, Anomia Kids is for ages 5 and up, and Anomia X is for adults only.

Sphero rates the BOLT+ for ages 8 and up and describes it as made for students in grades 3 to 10, so a 10 year old sits right in the middle of the range. It is a clear, waterproof ball packed with motors and sensors that rolls where the code tells it to go. It needs a tablet, phone or computer, and a 10 year old gets the most out of it by following the lessons instead of only driving it around. LEGO rates the Technic NASA Mars Rover Perseverance for ages 10 and up. The 1,132 pieces build a detailed model of the rover that landed on Mars in 2021, together with a small buildable Ingenuity helicopter. This is Technic, so it works like the real thing in miniature: 360 degree steering, a movable arm and a suspension that keeps all six wheels on the ground over uneven terrain.

A build of this size takes a 10 year old several sessions, which is exactly the point. Technic uses pins and beams instead of regular bricks, so it asks for more patience and careful reading of the instructions. An AR app shows the rover on Mars and explains the mission, and the LEGO Builder app has 3D instructions. On Amazon the set averages 4.8 stars from more than 1,300 ratings and is listed at $99.99. The set has been out since 2023, so check that the listing is the new official set and not a light kit or wall mount, which are sold separately.

Splendor from Space Cowboys is rated for ages 10 and up and is for 2 to 4 players. On each turn a player takes gem tokens or uses them to buy a card. Every card bought gives a permanent discount on later cards, so a slow start turns into a fast engine. The first player to reach 15 prestige points wins. A game takes about 30 minutes.

The box holds 40 weighty gem tokens, 90 development cards and 10 noble tiles. Unlike CATAN, it plays well with only two people, which makes it a good pick for an only child and a parent. There is little talking or trading, so kids who like noisy games may prefer Anomia. Do not confuse it with Splendor Duel, a separate 2 player game, or Splendor Kids for ages 6 and up.

The National Geographic Hobby Rock Tumbler Kit has a maker minimum age of 8 years, and at 10 a child has the patience this hobby asks for. The kit contains an electric tumbler with a rubber barrel, a supply of rough gemstones, four grades of polishing grit, jewelry fastenings and a learning guide. Kids load the barrel with rocks, water and grit and let it turn for days. Each of the four stages makes the stones smoother, until they come out shiny.

The whole process takes several weeks, and the changing of grit between stages is the part kids do themselves. A timer switches the machine off automatically. The finished stones can be glued onto the included ring and necklace fastenings. Buyers are happy with the results and warn about the noise: the barrel turns day and night, so most families put it in a garage, basement or laundry room. Refill packs with rocks and grit are sold separately. It plugs into a regular 120 volt outlet.

Buying guide: how to choose the right gifts for 10 year olds

Check the maker age

Every gift here has a maker age rating that includes 10 year olds. Seven are rated 10 and up, which means the rules, the reading and the fine motor work are aimed at this age. The other three are rated 8 and up but are built to last well past 10. Small parts stay a risk for younger brothers and sisters.

What 10 year olds are ready for

At 10, kids can plan many steps ahead, negotiate, follow a long manual and stick with a project for weeks. That opens the door to strategy games such as CATAN and Splendor, Technic sets with over a thousand pieces, text coding and real chemistry. Toys that feel childish tend to stay in the box.

Solo gifts and group gifts

Think about when the gift will be used. The Perplexus, the LEGO Technic rover, the string art kit and the rock tumbler are solo projects. CATAN, Anomia and Risk need at least two or three players. Splendor works well with just a parent and child.

Devices, power and adult help

The Sphero BOLT+ needs a tablet, phone or computer. The rock tumbler plugs into a wall outlet and runs for days. The chemistry set requires adult supervision. None of the other gifts need batteries, which is one less thing to buy before the gift is unwrapped.

Order in time for Christmas

Black Friday is November 27, 2026 and Cyber Monday is November 30. Board games and LEGO sets are often discounted that week, and popular ones can sell out in December. Order by mid December for delivery before December 25, and earlier if you ship to family in another state.

Gift receipts and returns

Add a gift receipt without prices at checkout. Most items shipped and sold by Amazon in November and December can be returned until the end of January, which helps when two relatives pick the same gift. Check the return window on the product page.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best gift for a 10 year old?

For most 10 year olds, CATAN is the best choice. It is rated for ages 10 and up, is easy to learn and gets played for years. On a budget, the card game Anomia costs about $17 and is also rated 10+.

What is a good Christmas gift for a 10 year old?

Popular Christmas gifts for a 10 year old are CATAN, Splendor, the LEGO Technic NASA Mars Rover Perseverance, the National Geographic Hobby Rock Tumbler and the Perplexus Epic. All have a maker age rating that fits a 10 year old.

What are good STEM gifts for a 10 year old?

The Sphero BOLT+ teaches coding from blocks to Python, the Thames & Kosmos Chem C500 is a first real chemistry set rated 10+, and the LEGO Technic Mars Rover shows how steering and suspension work. The rock tumbler adds geology.

What do you get a 10 year old who has everything?

Pick something that takes time or other people: a rock tumbler that runs for weeks, a Perplexus Epic that takes practice to finish, or a game such as CATAN or Anomia that a 10 year old plays with family and friends.

Are these gifts good for both boys and girls?

Yes. Strategy games, coding robots, LEGO Technic, science kits and craft kits are popular with 10 year old boys and girls alike. Choose by interest: games, building, science, coding or crafts.

How much should I spend on a gift for a 10 year old?

Good gifts for 10 year olds start around $13 for a string art kit and $17 for Anomia. Board games and science kits cost $28 to $50, the rock tumbler about $70, the LEGO Technic rover about $100 and the Sphero BOLT+ about $180.
