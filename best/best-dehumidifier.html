The 10 Best Dehumidifiers of 2026

A dehumidifier pulls moisture out of the air and keeps a basement, bedroom or laundry room below the 50 percent humidity where mold and dust mites thrive. Most US models run on a standard 120-volt outlet and are rated in pints per day under the 2019 DOE test, so a 50-pint unit is the usual pick for basements and large rooms. We compared the dehumidifiers on Amazon on real capacity, tank size, drain options, smart controls and energy use, checked prices and read thousands of buyer reviews.

Buying guide: how to choose the right dehumidifiers

What humidity is healthy?

Indoor humidity between 30 and 50 percent is the comfortable and healthy range. Above about 60 percent, mold, mildew and dust mites grow fast, wood swells and basements start to smell musty. A dehumidifier with a built-in humidistat lets you set a target, often 45 or 50 percent, and switches the compressor on and off to hold it. A cheap separate hygrometer is useful to check the reading in different rooms.

How many pints do you need?

Since 2019, US dehumidifiers are rated under a stricter DOE test at 65 degrees Fahrenheit, so a model sold as 50 pints today would have been called 70 pints before. As a rule of thumb, 20 to 25 pints suits a single damp room, 35 pints a medium space or finished basement, and 50 pints a large or wet basement. Buying a size up is smart: a bigger unit reaches the target faster and then cycles off.

Bucket, hose or pump?

Every unit here has a bucket with an automatic shutoff, but in a damp basement a 50-pint unit can fill it once or twice a day. If you have a floor drain, a hose lets water run out continuously. If the water has to go up to a sink or out a window, choose a model with a built-in pump, such as the Honeywell TPFIT50PWK or the GE pump model. Crawl space units like the AlorAir have no bucket at all.

Running costs and ENERGY STAR

A 50-pint dehumidifier draws about 500 watts while the compressor runs. Because it cycles with the humidistat, a typical basement unit uses between 2 and 6 kWh a day in the humid season. ENERGY STAR models remove more water per kWh, which adds up over a summer. Keeping doors and windows closed, cleaning the filter monthly and setting 50 percent instead of 35 percent all cut the bill.

Cold basements and crawl spaces

Standard compressor dehumidifiers lose efficiency below about 65 degrees Fahrenheit and can ice up near 41 degrees. Look for auto defrost if your basement is unheated. For crawl spaces and spaces that stay cold and very damp, a dedicated crawl space unit such as the AlorAir Sentinel is built to run nonstop at low temperatures and is rated under AHAM conditions.

Noise and placement

Most 50-pint units run at roughly 45 to 55 decibels on the low fan, about as loud as a refrigerator. Place the unit a few inches from the wall so air can flow through the filter, ideally in the middle of the damp area. In a bedroom, a model with a sleep mode or a display that turns off, such as the Soleus or the Midea Cube, is easier to live with.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best dehumidifier in 2026?

Based on our comparison of specs, prices and buyer reviews, the Midea Cube 50 Pint is the best dehumidifier for most homes thanks to its large tank, quiet running and smart controls. The TCL H50D44W is the best value.

What size dehumidifier do I need for a basement?

For most basements a 50-pint model under the current DOE rating is the right choice. Smaller units of 20 to 35 pints suit single rooms or a finished basement that is only slightly damp. A very wet or cold crawl space needs a dedicated crawl space dehumidifier.

Does a dehumidifier help against mold?

Yes, keeping humidity below 50 percent stops most mold and mildew from growing. A dehumidifier does not remove existing mold, so clean affected areas first and fix leaks or drainage problems that cause the moisture.

How much electricity does a dehumidifier use?

A 50-pint unit draws about 500 watts while running. Because it switches on and off with the humidistat, daily use is often 2 to 6 kWh, depending on how damp the space is. ENERGY STAR models use less energy for the same amount of water removed.

Should I run my dehumidifier all the time?

Leave it on with a humidity setting of around 45 to 50 percent and let the humidistat decide when it runs. In very damp basements it may run most of the day in summer and hardly at all in winter, when indoor air is naturally drier.

Do I need a dehumidifier with a pump?

Only if you want continuous drainage and have no floor drain at a lower level. A pump pushes water up through a hose to a sink or window. With a floor drain, a simple gravity hose is enough, and without either you empty the bucket by hand.
