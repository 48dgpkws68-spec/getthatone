Buying guide: how to choose the right gifts for 1 year olds

Check the age on the box

Every toy here is rated by its maker for 1 year olds. That rating is about safety as much as skill: toys for ages 3 and up can contain small parts that are a choking risk for a 12 month old. If you buy something not on this list, look for an age range that includes 12 months on the box or the Amazon listing, and skip anything labeled 3+.

What 1 year olds love to do

At 12 to 18 months, most kids pull up, cruise along furniture and take their first steps. They love cause and effect: press a button, hear a sound. They fill and empty containers, stack and knock down, and bang on anything that makes noise. The best gifts tap into one of those urges, like a push walker, a pounding bench or a set of big blocks.

Walkers: push toys, not seat walkers

The walkers in this list are push walkers: your child stands behind them and pushes. Seated baby walkers with wheels are discouraged by the American Academy of Pediatrics because of fall risks. For a beginner, pick a push walker that rolls slowly, such as the VTech with its speed switch or the Radio Flyer with resistance clickers.

Wood or plastic, batteries or not

Wooden toys like the Hape bench and the Melissa & Doug sorter are quiet, sturdy and look nice in a living room. Electronic toys like the VTech cube offer more to discover and teach words and numbers. Many parents like a mix. Check whether batteries are included: the Baby Einstein piano needs 3 AA batteries that are not in the box.

Think about the next year

A 1 year old grows fast. Toys with a wide age range, like Mega Bloks (1 to 5 years), the Little People farm (1 to 5 years) or the Radio Flyer wagon (1 to 4 years), give more play per dollar than toys that are outgrown in a few months.

Order early for Christmas

Popular toddler toys sell out or rise in price in December. Order at least a week before Christmas or a first birthday party, and add a gift receipt so parents can swap a duplicate. Amazon often extends holiday returns into January.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best gift for a 1 year old?

For most 1 year olds, the VTech Sit-to-Stand Learning Walker is the best gift. VTech rates it for 9 to 36 months, it supports first steps and the play panel also works on the floor. It costs about $30.

What is a good Christmas gift for a 1 year old?

A sturdy push walker like the Radio Flyer Classic Walker Wagon makes a big Christmas gift for a 1 year old. Smaller favorites are the Hape Pound & Tap Bench, the Fisher-Price Little People farm and Mega Bloks. All are rated by their makers for 1 year olds.

What toys are safe for a 12 month old?

Choose toys whose maker gives an age range that includes 12 months, and avoid anything labeled 3+. Those can contain small parts that are a choking hazard. All ten toys in this list are rated for 1 year olds.

How much should I spend on a gift for a 1 year old?

Between $15 and $35 buys a great gift, such as the VTech Drop and Go Dump Truck, Mega Bloks or the Hape bench. A 1 year old will not care about the price. A bigger budget of about $100 buys something built to last, like the Radio Flyer Classic Walker Wagon.

Are wooden toys better for 1 year olds?

Not necessarily. Wooden toys are quiet and durable, and many parents like how they look. Electronic toys can teach words, colors and numbers. A mix works well: for example a wooden pounding bench and a VTech activity cube.

Should a 1 year old use a baby walker?

Push walkers that your child stands behind are fine and help with first steps. Seated walkers with wheels are discouraged by pediatricians because of fall and injury risks. All walkers in this list are push walkers.
