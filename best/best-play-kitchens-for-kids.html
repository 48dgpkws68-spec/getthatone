Buying guide: how to choose the right play kitchens for kids

Check the maker's age rating

Every kitchen in this list has an age rating from the manufacturer: 2+ for the Step2 toddler kitchens and 3+ for most wooden models. A 3+ rating often means small parts or accessories that can be a choking risk for younger children. If a baby or toddler under 3 lives in the house, keep small play food and utensils out of reach.

Wood or plastic

Wooden kitchens from Melissa & Doug, KidKraft, Hape and Teamson look like furniture and suit a living room. Plastic kitchens from Step2 are lighter, easier to clean, usually include more accessories and can go outside. Wood takes longer to assemble, plastic looks more like a toy.

Measure your space

Sizes range from about 22 inches wide for the Hape Gourmet Kitchen to more than 8 feet for the Step2 Grand Walk-In. Measure the spot before you buy and leave room for a child to stand in front. Corner and dual-sided models need extra space but let several kids play at once.

Accessories are not always included

Step2 and Little Tikes kitchens come with 20 to 103 pieces. Most wooden kitchens include few or no pots, pans and food. Add about $20 to $40 for an accessory set if you choose a wooden model, and check the age rating on that set too.

Lights and sounds

Burners that glow and sizzle are popular with toddlers, but they need batteries, which are rarely included. Some parents prefer a quiet wooden kitchen without electronics. Both work: kids mostly play with the doors, knobs, sink and food.

Plan time for assembly

Wooden kitchens often take two to four hours to build, according to buyer reviews. Plastic Step2 kitchens are faster, except the Grand Walk-In. Assemble it before a birthday or the holidays, not on the morning itself.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best play kitchen for kids?

For most families, the Melissa & Doug Wooden Chef's Pretend Play Kitchen is the best choice. It is rated for ages 3 and up, has a fridge, freezer, ice dispenser, oven and microwave, and fits two kids at once. On a budget, the Step2 Best Chefs Kitchen costs about $90 and includes 25 accessories.

What age is a play kitchen for?

Most play kitchens are rated by the maker for ages 3 and up. Some Step2 kitchens, like the Best Chefs, Fun with Friends and Grand Walk-In, are rated from age 2. Children usually play with a kitchen until about age 6 or 7.

Is a wooden or plastic play kitchen better?

Wood looks nicer and feels sturdier, but takes longer to assemble and usually comes without accessories. Plastic is easier to clean, lighter and often includes 25 or more pieces. For toddlers, plastic is practical; for a long-lasting kitchen in the living room, wood is the better pick.

What is the best play kitchen for a 2 year old?

Choose a kitchen the maker rates for age 2, such as the Step2 Best Chefs Kitchen or the Step2 Fun with Friends Kitchen. Both have large parts, lights and sounds and 25 accessories. Most wooden kitchens are rated 3+.

How much should I spend on a play kitchen?

A good wooden kitchen starts around $60 with the Hape Gourmet Kitchen and the Teamson Little Chef Florence. Most full size kitchens cost $90 to $150. Large walk-in models like the Step2 Grand Walk-In cost about $300.

Do play kitchens come with accessories?

Plastic kitchens from Step2 and the Little Tikes Wood Chef's Kitchen include 20 to 103 pieces. Most wooden kitchens from Melissa & Doug, KidKraft and Hape include few or none, so plan for a separate cookware and food set.
