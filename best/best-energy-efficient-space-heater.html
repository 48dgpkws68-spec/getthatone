Buying guide: how to choose the right energy efficient space heaters

Are energy efficient space heaters real?

Every plug-in electric heater turns almost 100 percent of its power into heat, so no 1,500-watt model creates more heat from the same electricity. The savings come from how you use it: warm only the room you are in and set your central thermostat a few degrees lower. A precise digital thermostat and eco mode help by cycling the heater instead of running at full power.

What does a space heater cost to run?

At full power, a 1,500-watt heater uses 1.5 kWh per hour. At the average US rate of about 17 cents per kWh, that is roughly 25 cents per hour. With a thermostat cycling it on and off, real costs are often half that. Heating one room this way can be cheaper than heating the whole house, especially with electric baseboard or older furnaces.

Ceramic, oil-filled or fan heater?

Ceramic heaters warm up in seconds and suit quick, targeted heat. Oil-filled radiators are silent and give steady heat that lasts after they cycle off, ideal for bedrooms. Vortex fan heaters like Vornado circulate warm air evenly through a room. Infrared heaters warm people and objects directly and work well in drafty spaces.

Size your heater to the room

A 1,500-watt heater can heat about 150 square feet as primary heat, or a larger room as supplemental heat. Claims of heating 1,000 square feet assume an insulated home with central heat already running. For big open rooms, choose a circulating or infrared model.

Safety first

Look for tip-over shutoff, overheat protection and a UL or ETL listing. Plug the heater directly into a wall outlet, never into an extension cord or power strip. Keep it at least three feet from curtains, beds and furniture, and do not leave it running unattended or while you are away.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best energy efficient space heater in 2026?

Based on our comparison of specs, prices and buyer reviews, the Vornado VH200 is the best space heater for most rooms thanks to even whole-room heat and a reliable thermostat. The Dreo Atom One is the best value with a precise digital thermostat and eco mode.

Which type of space heater uses the least electricity?

All 1,500-watt heaters use the same power at full output. The one that uses the least is the one with the most accurate thermostat and eco mode, set to the lowest comfortable temperature. Oil-filled heaters can feel more efficient because they keep radiating heat after cycling off.

Is it cheaper to run a space heater or central heat?

Heating one room with a space heater while turning down central heat is usually cheaper, especially if your home uses electric resistance heat. If you have an efficient heat pump or gas furnace, heating the whole house may cost less per degree, so use space heaters for occupied rooms only.

Can I leave a space heater on overnight?

Many people do with modern heaters that have tip-over shutoff, overheat protection and a thermostat. An oil-filled radiator is the safest and quietest option for bedrooms. Keep it three feet from bedding and plug it directly into the wall.

Why does my space heater trip the breaker?

A 1,500-watt heater draws about 12.5 amps, which is most of a standard 15-amp circuit. If other devices share that circuit, the breaker trips. Use an outlet on a less loaded circuit or switch the heater to its low setting.

What is the safest space heater for a home with kids and pets?

Choose a heater with a cool-touch exterior, tip-over shutoff and no exposed elements, such as the Dyson Purifier Hot+Cool HP10 or Vornado VH200. Oil-filled radiators also work, but their surfaces get warm.
