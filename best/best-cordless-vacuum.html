Buying guide: how to choose the right cordless vacuums

Can a cordless vacuum replace an upright?

For most apartments and homes with hard floors and area rugs, yes. Powerful models like the Dyson V15 and V16 or Shark Stratos clean carpet nearly as well as an upright. In a large house with lots of thick wall-to-wall carpet, a corded upright still offers more constant power for deep cleaning.

Runtime: read the fine print

Advertised runtimes are measured on the lowest power with a non-motorized tool. With the floor head on a normal setting, expect roughly half that, and on max power often 10 to 15 minutes. A swappable battery lets you double runtime with a spare. For a home over 1,500 square feet, look for at least 40 minutes on normal power.

Pet hair and tangles

Long hair wraps around brushrolls and kills suction. Look for an anti-tangle or self-cleaning brushroll, like Dyson's Motorbar or Shark's self-cleaning roller. A motorized mini tool is essential for pet beds, couches and stairs.

Weight and balance

Most stick vacuums weigh 5 to 8.5 pounds. Lighter models are easier on stairs and overhead, but heavier models usually clean carpet better. Dysons have the motor near the handle, which feels top-heavy; Shark places more weight lower, which helps on floors but less in handheld mode.

Filters and allergies

If someone in your home has allergies, look for sealed HEPA filtration that traps fine dust instead of leaking it out. Wash or replace filters as recommended, usually once a month. Auto-empty bases like Shark's reduce the dust cloud when emptying.

Bin size and emptying

Bins range from under 0.1 gallon to about 0.26 gallon. Small bins need emptying after every session in pet homes. Hygienic emptying systems that push dirt out, or self-emptying bases, keep your hands clean.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best cordless vacuum in 2026?

Based on our comparison of specs, prices and buyer reviews, the Dyson V15 Detect Plus is the best cordless vacuum for most homes. It combines strong suction, an anti-tangle head and smart dust sensing. The Levoit LVAC-200 is the best value.

Which cordless vacuum is best for pet hair?

The Dyson V15 Detect and V16 Piston Animal are the strongest, while the Shark PowerDetect with its self-cleaning brushroll and auto-empty base is ideal for pet owners who hate emptying bins. On a budget, the Shark Rocket Pet Pro works well.

How long do cordless vacuum batteries last?

Lithium-ion batteries usually last three to five years before runtime drops noticeably. Many Dyson, Shark and Levoit models have replaceable or swappable batteries, so you do not have to replace the whole vacuum.

Is a Dyson worth the money?

If you have carpet, pets or allergies, a Dyson V15 or V16 delivers excellent suction, filtration and smart features. For hard floors in a small home, a cheaper model like the Levoit LVAC-200 cleans nearly as well.

Are cordless vacuums good on carpet?

Premium cordless vacuums handle carpet well thanks to motorized brushrolls and high suction. Budget models are best on hard floors and low-pile rugs. For thick carpet, choose a Dyson V15, V16 or Shark Stratos.

How often should I clean the filter?

Rinse washable filters about once a month and let them dry fully for 24 hours before reinstalling. Clean them more often if you have pets or vacuum fine dust.
