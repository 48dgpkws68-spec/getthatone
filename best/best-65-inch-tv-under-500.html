Buying guide: how to choose the right 65 inch TV under 500

What $500 buys at 65 inches

At this budget you choose between three panel types. Basic LED sets like the Insignia F50 and LG UA77 are the cheapest. QLED models such as the Hisense E6, QD6 and Roku Select Series add a quantum dot layer for richer color. Mini LED with local dimming, found on the TCL QM6K and Hisense U6, is the real step up: darker blacks and brighter HDR. If you can reach $450 to $500, Mini LED is worth it.

Is 65 inches the right size?

A 65-inch TV suits a viewing distance of about 7 to 9 feet, which matches most living room couches. Measure the wall or stand first: a 65-inch set is about 57 inches wide. If you sit closer than 6 feet, our guide to the best 55-inch TVs under $500 may fit better, with more premium models for the same money.

HDR formats

Netflix and Disney+ mostly stream in Dolby Vision, and Prime Video uses HDR10+. The TCL QM6K, Hisense U6, Hisense E6, Hisense QD6 and Roku Select Series support both. The Hisense E6SR has Dolby Vision only, Samsung has HDR10+ only, and the LG UA77 and Insignia stop at HDR10. Brightness and local dimming matter more than the format list, though.

Gaming at 65 inches

Only the TCL QM6K and Hisense U6 have native 144Hz panels, so they are the picks for a PS5, Xbox Series X or gaming PC at 120 frames per second. Every other TV here is native 60Hz. A Motion Rate of 120 or 240 on the box is processing, not a faster screen. Look for VRR and ALLM to reduce tearing and lag.

Fire TV, Google TV, Roku, Tizen or webOS?

All have the major US apps. Google TV on the TCL is the most complete and has Chromecast built in. Fire TV works best with Alexa but shows more promotions. Roku OS is the simplest for kids and older relatives. Samsung Tizen and LG webOS are quick and include free live channels. AirPlay is built into most models here.

Prices move around

65-inch TV prices on Amazon change often, especially around Black Friday. The TCL QM6K and Samsung M70H only fit this guide at their usual sale prices, while the Hisense E6 and QD6 often drop well below $400. Check the current price and exact model number before you buy, and skip renewed listings unless you want one on purpose.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best 65-inch TV under $500?

The TCL QM6K. It has a Mini LED backlight with local dimming, a native 144Hz panel, Dolby Vision and HDR10+ on Google TV, and it usually sells for about $500 on Amazon.

Can you get a 65-inch Mini LED TV under $500?

Yes. The TCL QM6K and Hisense U6 both have Mini LED with local dimming for $450 to $500. The Samsung M70H is Mini LED too, but only fits the budget on sale.

What is the cheapest good 65-inch TV?

The Hisense QD6 gives you QLED color and Dolby Vision for about $350. If you only need the basics, the Insignia F50 costs around $300.

Which 65-inch TV under $500 is best for gaming?

The TCL QM6K or Hisense U6. Both have native 144Hz panels with VRR and ALLM, so a PS5 or Xbox Series X can run at 120 frames per second.

Is a cheap 65-inch TV better than a premium 55-inch TV?

If you sit 7 feet or more away, a good 65-inch like the TCL QM6K usually feels better than a smaller premium set. Closer up, a 55-inch with better contrast can be the smarter buy.
