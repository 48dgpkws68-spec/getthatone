Buying guide: how to choose the right tech gifts under $100

Match the gift to daily habits

The best tech gifts fit something the person already does every day. Earbuds for a commuter, a mouse for a remote worker, a tracker for someone who wants to sleep better. A gadget for a hobby they do not have often ends up in a drawer.

Check the phone and ecosystem

Some gadgets only work with one platform. The AirTag needs an iPhone, and smart displays work best with the voice assistant someone already uses. If you are not sure, pick something universal like a Bluetooth speaker, a power bank or wired headphones.

Watch the hidden costs

An instant camera needs film, some fitness features need a subscription and a tracker may need a holder. Add these to the budget, or include the first film pack or accessory with the gift so it can be used right away on Christmas morning.

Buy early for holiday deals

Many of these products drop in price around Black Friday and Cyber Monday. Popular colors can sell out in December, so order in time and check delivery dates for your area. Keep the gift receipt in case of a size or color swap.

Plug and power in the US

All picks here use USB-C, batteries or a 120 V adapter included in the box, so nothing extra is needed. Check whether a USB wall charger is included, since many gadgets ship with a cable only. A cheap USB-C charger makes a nice add-on.

How we picked

We did not test these products ourselves. We compared specifications, list prices and thousands of buyer reviews on Amazon and selected well-known models that are sold new. Prices change often, so check the current price before you buy.
