Buying guide: how to choose the right OLED TV

OLED, QD-OLED or tandem OLED?

Every OLED pixel gives its own light and can switch off fully, which gives true black. Standard OLED panels, as in the LG B6, are the cheapest. LG's OLED evo panels are brighter. QD-OLED, as in the Sony BRAVIA 8 II, adds quantum dots for brighter colors. Four-layer tandem panels, as in the LG G5 and Panasonic Z95B, are among the brightest. In a dim room all of them look excellent.

Which size do you need?

In the US, 65 inches is the most popular size for a living room and it fits a viewing distance of about 7 to 9 feet. Pick 55 inches for a smaller room and 48 inches for a bedroom or desk. Most models in this list are sold in several sizes, often up to 77 or 83 inches. A larger size of a cheaper series often gives more joy than a smaller flagship.

Bright room or dark room

OLED is at its best in a dim room. If your living room has a lot of daylight, look at a brighter panel such as the LG G5 or a glare-free screen such as the Samsung S90H. If you mostly watch in the evening, an entry model such as the LG B6 gives nearly the same experience for much less money.

Gaming: HDMI 2.1 and refresh rate

For a PS5 or Xbox Series X you want 4K at 120Hz with VRR, which needs HDMI 2.1. LG and Samsung offer four such ports on the models in this list. The Sony BRAVIA 8 II and the Panasonic Z95B have two. Refresh rates of 144Hz or 165Hz only matter with a gaming PC. Consoles stop at 120Hz.

Dolby Vision or HDR10+

LG and Sony support Dolby Vision, which Netflix, Disney+ and Apple TV+ use widely. Samsung does not support Dolby Vision and uses HDR10+, which Prime Video offers. The Panasonic Z95B supports both. Every TV here plays standard HDR10, so you always get an HDR picture. Movie fans who want the best version of each title should weigh this point.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best OLED TV in 2026?

For most people the LG C6 is the best OLED TV of 2026. It combines an OLED evo panel, four HDMI 2.1 ports and Dolby Vision at a price well below launch. For the best movie picture, the Sony BRAVIA 8 II is the premium choice.

Is an OLED TV worth it?

Yes, if you care about picture quality. OLED gives true black, very high contrast and wide viewing angles. That shows most with movies and series in a dim room. Entry models such as the LG B6 have made OLED much more affordable than a few years ago.

Should I wait for Black Friday to buy an OLED TV?

Usually yes. Black Friday is November 27, 2026, and OLED TVs are often at their lowest price of the year around that date. Outgoing models such as the LG C5 can sell out, so do not wait too long if you see a good price.

Is burn-in still a problem with OLED?

The risk is small with normal, varied viewing. Current OLED TVs use pixel shifting and automatic panel care to prevent it. Static images for many hours a day, such as a news ticker or a game HUD at full brightness, remain a risk. LG gives a 5-year panel warranty on its G series.

Which is better, the LG C6 or the Samsung S90H?

The LG C6 supports Dolby Vision and has four HDMI 2.1 ports. The Samsung S90H has a glare-free screen and a 165Hz mode but no Dolby Vision. Pick the Samsung for a bright room and the LG if you stream a lot of Dolby Vision movies.

What is the best cheap OLED TV?

The LG B6 is the best value among current models, with a 65 inch version reported around $1,200. The LG C5 from 2025 is a strong deal while stock lasts. For a smaller budget and room, the 55 inch Samsung S85H is an option.
