We compared ten popular gifts for 8 year olds on Amazon, from Snap Circuits and the Rubik's Cube to Ticket to Ride, LEGO Creator 3 in 1 and the mBot2 robot, on price, specs and buyer reviews.

Elenco rates the Snap Circuits SC-100 for ages 8 and up, and it has been a favorite first electronics kit for years. Kids snap color coded parts onto a plastic grid to build working circuits. The full color manual covers more than 100 projects, including a flying saucer, a musical doorbell, a voice controlled lamp, a sound activated switch and a police siren with light.

Nothing needs tools or soldering, and the manual shows every build as a picture, so an 8 year old can read and follow it without a parent. That independence is what buyers praise most, along with how sturdy the parts are after years of use. The kit needs 2 AA batteries, which are not included. When all projects are done, Elenco sells upgrade kits that turn the SC-100 into the larger SC-300 or Pro sets, so the gift keeps growing. Amazon listings mention 28 to 32 parts depending on the edition.

The Rubik's Cube from Spin Master is rated for ages 8 and up, and 8 is a great age to start. A child this age can memorize the short move sequences needed to solve the cube layer by layer. The current official 3x3 has a sticker free design with colored tiles, so nothing peels off, and Amazon listings describe smoother turning than the cubes parents grew up with.

Most kids need a guide for the first solve. Rubik's offers free step by step instructions and videos, and there is also a Coach version of the cube with learning stickers. Buyers say the first full solve often takes a few days of practice, and after that kids start timing themselves and racing friends. It needs no batteries, fits in a coat pocket and survives being dropped. As a stocking gift or an add-on to a bigger present, it is hard to beat for the price.

Makeblock lists the mBot2 with a minimum age of 8 years, and it is a big step up from toy robots with a remote. Kids first build the robot, which takes about 30 minutes according to the Amazon listing, and then program it with drag and drop blocks based on Scratch. When they are older, the same robot can be coded in Python, so it stays useful into middle school.

The mBot2 comes with the CyberPi controller, connects over WiFi and includes a rechargeable battery, so there is no stack of AA batteries to buy. The older first generation mBot is cheaper but runs on 4 AA batteries and only supports Scratch and Arduino. Parents say an 8 year old needs help with the app and the first lessons, and that kids take over quickly after that. It needs a tablet or computer. For a child who already likes Scratch at school, this is the gift that makes code move.

Buying guide: how to choose the right gifts for 8 year olds

Check the age on the box

Every gift here is rated by its maker for ages 8 and up. That rating tells you the instructions, the fine motor work and the rules fit an 8 year old. It also means small parts: marbles, LEGO bricks and game pieces should stay away from younger brothers and sisters.

What 8 year olds are ready for

Most 8 year olds read well enough to follow a manual alone, can plan several steps ahead and enjoy games with real strategy. That is why electronics kits, coding robots, logic puzzles and family board games suddenly work at this age, while many toys rated 5+ start to feel babyish.

Solo gifts and family gifts

Think about when the gift will be used. Snap Circuits, the Rubik's Cube, Gravity Maze, LEGO and Spirograph are things a child does alone. Ticket to Ride and Sleeping Queens need other players. A good gift list has at least one of each.

Batteries, devices and adult help

Snap Circuits Jr. needs 2 AA batteries, not included. The mBot2 has a rechargeable battery but needs a tablet or computer for coding. The crystal lab needs very hot water, so an adult has to help with the first step. Check these before the gift is unwrapped.

Order in time for Christmas

Black Friday is November 27, 2026 and Cyber Monday November 30. Popular games and LEGO sets can sell out in December. Order by mid December for delivery before December 25, and earlier if you ship to family in another state.

Gift receipts and returns

Add a gift receipt without prices at checkout. Most items shipped and sold by Amazon in November and December can be returned until the end of January, which helps when two relatives pick the same gift. Check the return window on the product page.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best gift for an 8 year old?

For most 8 year olds, Snap Circuits Jr. SC-100 is the best choice. It is rated for ages 8 and up and has more than 100 electronics projects kids can build alone. On a budget, the Rubik's Cube 3x3 costs about $10.

What is a good Christmas gift for an 8 year old?

Popular Christmas gifts for an 8 year old are Ticket to Ride, ThinkFun Gravity Maze, a LEGO Creator 3 in 1 set, the Spirograph Deluxe Set and the National Geographic Mega Crystal Growing Lab. All are rated by the maker for ages 8 and up.

What are good STEM gifts for an 8 year old?

Snap Circuits Jr. teaches electronics, the Makeblock mBot2 teaches coding in Scratch, Gravity Maze builds logic and the National Geographic crystal lab is a first chemistry experiment. All four are rated 8+.

What do you get an 8 year old who has everything?

Pick something that takes time to master, like a Rubik's Cube or Spirograph, or a game the family plays together, like Ticket to Ride or Sleeping Queens. These get used long after the holidays.

Can an 8 year old learn to code with a robot?

Yes. The Makeblock mBot2 has a maker minimum age of 8 years and uses drag and drop blocks based on Scratch. Expect to help with the app and the first lessons. It needs a tablet or computer.

How much should I spend on a gift for an 8 year old?

Good gifts for 8 year olds start around $10 for a Rubik's Cube or a card game. LEGO Creator sets cost about $20, science kits and logic games $25 to $35, Ticket to Ride about $55 and the mBot2 about $140.
