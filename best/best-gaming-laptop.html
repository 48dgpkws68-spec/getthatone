Buying guide: how to choose the right gaming laptop

Start with the graphics chip

The graphics chip decides your frame rate. An RTX 5050 or RTX 5060 is fine for full HD. The RTX 5070 Ti with 12GB is the sweet spot for sharper 2560 x 1600 screens. An RTX 5080 or RTX 5090 is for the highest settings and costs far more. Pay attention to video memory: 8GB is tight for new games at high textures.

Match the screen to the chip

A 2560 x 1600 screen at 240Hz only pays off with an RTX 5070 Ti or better. With an RTX 5060, a 1920 x 1200 screen at 165Hz or 180Hz gives smoother play. OLED gives deeper black and faster response. A bright IPS screen is cheaper and has no risk of burn-in from static images.

Memory and storage

16GB of memory is the minimum, 32GB is comfortable. Big games take 100GB or more, so 1TB of storage is the practical starting point. Most models in this list have a free slot for a second SSD and memory you can swap. The Razer Blade 16 is the exception, with soldered memory.

Watch the Amazon listing

Many gaming laptops on Amazon are sold by resellers who add memory, storage or accessories and change the Windows version. That can be a fair deal, but compare with the standard model, check who the seller is and ask how the warranty works. The title should name the exact graphics chip.

Weight, noise and battery

Powerful laptops are heavy and loud under load, and most last only an hour or two while gaming on battery. Plan to play plugged in. If you carry the laptop every day, a thinner model such as the Razer Blade 16 or a lighter RTX 5060 laptop is easier to live with.

Black Friday timing

Black Friday is November 27, 2026 and Cyber Monday is November 30. Gaming laptops often drop $200 to $700 in that week. Prices in this guide are indicative and change daily, so note the price of your pick now and compare it when the deals start.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best gaming laptop in 2026?

For most people the ASUS ROG Strix G16 with RTX 5070 Ti is the best choice. It has 12GB of video memory, a 2560 x 1600 screen at 240Hz and 32GB of memory for about $2,400, and often less during sales.

What is the best budget gaming laptop?

The Lenovo LOQ 15 with RTX 5060 is the best value at about $1,200. If you want to stay under $1,000, the HP Victus 15 with RTX 5050 runs popular games well at full HD.

Is 8GB of video memory enough for gaming?

For full HD, yes. At higher resolutions and the highest texture settings, some new games ask for more than 8GB. If you want a laptop that stays at high settings for years, choose an RTX 5070 Ti with 12GB or better.

Is Black Friday a good time to buy a gaming laptop?

Yes. Gaming laptops are among the most discounted products in the week of Black Friday, November 27, 2026. Discounts of $200 to $700 are common, especially on models that have been out for some months.

How much memory does a gaming laptop need?

16GB is the minimum for new games. 32GB is better if you stream, keep many browser tabs open or want the laptop to last. On most models you can add memory later, except thin laptops with soldered memory.

Is an OLED screen worth it on a gaming laptop?

OLED gives true black, strong contrast and very fast response, which looks great in games and movies. It costs more and static images can leave marks over time. A good IPS screen is the safer choice if you also work long days on the laptop.
