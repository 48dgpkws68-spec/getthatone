We compared gaming laptops that are for sale on Amazon on graphics power, processor, screen, memory, storage, weight and current reference prices from US price comparison sites. Verdict

Our conclusion

The MSI Vector 16 HX AI with RTX 5070 Ti is the best gaming laptop for most people right now: 12GB of video memory, 32GB of memory and a 240Hz screen for about $2,200, roughly $600 less than our former top pick, the ASUS ROG Strix G16. The Acer Nitro V 17 with RTX 5060 is the best value at about $1,050. If money matters less, the Razer Blade 16 with RTX 5090 is the premium pick in a thin aluminum body. For RTX 5080 speed, look for the 32GB version of the Lenovo Legion Pro 7i.

When not to buy

  • If your budget is under $1,000. On November 26, 2026 the cheapest laptop in this list is the Acer Nitro V 17 with RTX 5060 at $1,049.99, and the Acer Nitro V 15 with the slower RTX 5050 was listed at $1,086. Tom's Hardware calls a gaming laptop under $1,000 with a current graphics chip rare. If $1,000 is your limit, wait for a deal instead of stretching to an older model at a new-model price.
  • An RTX 5070 Ti or better if you only play at full HD. Notebookcheck measured 88.5 frames per second in Cyberpunk 2077 at full HD and Ultra settings on the Lenovo LOQ 15 with RTX 5060, and 119.6 on the MSI Vector 16 HX AI with RTX 5070 Ti. Both are smooth. The faster chip costs $700 to $1,150 more in this list and only pays off on a 2560 x 1600 screen or at 240Hz.
  • A thick 16 inch model if you game away from a power outlet. In Tom's Hardware's battery run of browsing, video and light graphics work, the Lenovo Legion Pro 7i lasted 4 hours and 37 minutes on a 99Wh battery. The Razer Blade 16 lasted 12 hours and 46 minutes in the same kind of run. While gaming, every laptop here needs its charger.
  • If you carry it every day or share a quiet room. Most 16 inch models in this list weigh about 5.8 pounds, and Notebookcheck weighed the power supplies of the MSI Vector and the Legion Pro 7i at 2.2 and 2.7 pounds on top of that. The same site measured 60 dB(A) from the MSI Vector in its fastest fan mode and 47 dB(A) in the normal modes.

Buying guide: how to choose the right gaming laptop

Start with the graphics chip

The graphics chip decides your frame rate. An RTX 5050 or RTX 5060 is fine for full HD. The RTX 5070 Ti with 12GB is the sweet spot for sharper 2560 x 1600 screens. An RTX 5080 or RTX 5090 is for the highest settings and costs far more. Pay attention to video memory: 8GB is tight for new games at high textures.

Match the screen to the chip

A 2560 x 1600 screen at 240Hz only pays off with an RTX 5070 Ti or better. With an RTX 5060, a full HD class screen at 165Hz gives smoother play. OLED gives deeper black and faster response. A bright IPS screen is cheaper and has no risk of burn-in from static images.

Memory and storage cost more in 2026

16GB of memory is the minimum, 32GB is comfortable. In 2026 the price gap between those versions grew: the MSI Vector 16 HX AI was listed at $1,799 with 16GB and $2,199 with 32GB. Most models in this list have two memory slots and room for a second SSD, so a cheaper version that you upgrade later can make sense. The Razer Blade 16 is the exception, with soldered memory.

Watch the Amazon listing

Many gaming laptops on Amazon are sold by resellers who add memory, storage or accessories and change the Windows version. That can be a fair deal, but compare with the standard model, check who the seller is and ask how the warranty works. The title should name the exact graphics chip, memory and storage. Stock also changes fast: several models in this guide were flagged as out of stock on November 26.

Weight, noise and battery

Powerful laptops are heavy and loud under load. Most 16 inch models here weigh about 5.8 pounds before you add a power brick of 2 pounds or more, and review sites measured 47 to 60 dB(A) of fan noise while gaming. Plan to play plugged in. If you carry the laptop every day, the 4.7 pound Razer Blade 16 or a lighter RTX 5060 laptop is easier to live with.

Is 8GB of video memory enough for gaming?

For full HD, yes. At higher resolutions and the highest texture settings, some new games ask for more than 8GB. If you want a laptop that stays at high settings for years, choose an RTX 5070 Ti with 12GB or better.

How much memory does a gaming laptop need?

16GB is the minimum for new games. 32GB is better if you stream, keep many browser tabs open or want the laptop to last. On most models you can add memory later, except thin laptops with soldered memory such as the Razer Blade 16.
