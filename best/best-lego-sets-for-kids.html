Buying guide: how to choose the right LEGO sets for kids

Go by the age on the box, then look at your kid

Every LEGO box shows the age the maker recommends, from 1.5+ for DUPLO to 10+ for the Grogu model in this list. That age says how hard the build is. A kid who builds a lot can often handle a set one step up, with some help. A first-time builder is happier with a set at or below their age. Regular LEGO contains small parts and is not for children under 3.

DUPLO, Classic or a theme?

DUPLO is for toddlers from 18 months. Classic boxes hold loose bricks without a fixed model and suit ages 4 and up. Themed sets such as City, Friends, Minecraft or Harry Potter give a model, figures and a story. Kids who love to follow instructions enjoy a themed set most. Kids who invent their own things get more out of a Classic box.

Play set or display model

Some sets are made to play with: the Friends tree house, the City prison island and the Knight Bus have figures and moving parts. Others, such as Grogu, are mainly for the shelf. Ask yourself what happens after the build. For kids under 9, a set with minifigures and a vehicle usually gets the most hours of play.

What do you pay per brick?

A common rule of thumb is about 10 cents per brick. The Classic box and the Creator pirate ship in this list are well under that. Licensed themes such as Star Wars, Harry Potter and Disney tend to cost more per brick. Sets with many figures or large parts, such as DUPLO, are also pricier per piece. Use it as a rough check, not a hard rule.

Order in time for Christmas

Black Friday is November 27, 2026 and Cyber Monday November 30. LEGO is often discounted on Amazon in that week, but popular sets sell out in December. Order by mid December for delivery before December 25. Amazon lets you add a gift receipt without prices, which helps if two people buy the same set.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best LEGO set for kids in 2026?

For most kids, the LEGO Friends Friendship Tree House Hangout (42652) is the best pick. It has 701 pieces, is rated 8+ by LEGO and comes with four minidolls, an elevator and a slide. For younger kids from 4, the Classic Large Creative Brick Box is the better choice.

At what age can a child start with LEGO?

Kids can start with LEGO DUPLO from 18 months. The bricks are large and made for small hands. Regular LEGO starts at age 4 with Classic boxes and sets marked 4+. Regular LEGO contains small parts and is not suitable for children under 3.

What does the age on a LEGO box mean?

The age on the box is LEGO's recommendation for how hard the build is. A 7+ set has simpler steps than a 10+ set. It is not a strict limit: experienced builders can go one step up with help. The warning for children under 3 is about small parts and is a safety rule.

Which LEGO set is good for a kid who has never had LEGO?

The Classic Large Creative Brick Box (10698) is a good first set from age 4. It has 790 bricks in 33 colors, a storage tub and no fixed model. For a child who wants figures and a story, add a small themed set such as the Minecraft Armadillo Mine Expedition from age 8.

Do LEGO sets from different themes fit together?

Yes. All regular LEGO bricks fit together, whether they come from City, Friends, Minecraft or Star Wars. Technic uses beams and pins but connects to regular bricks too. DUPLO bricks are twice the size and also fit on regular LEGO bricks.

What is a good LEGO set under $30?

The Technic Monster Jam Grave Digger Fire and Ice costs about $20 and is rated 7+. The Minecraft Armadillo Mine Expedition costs about $30 and is rated 8+. Both are complete sets with a clear play feature: a pull-back motor and a TNT lever.
