Buying guide: how to choose the right TVs under $1,000

What does $1,000 buy in 2026?

Under $1,000 you can choose between three kinds of TV: a 55-inch OLED such as the LG B5 or Samsung S85F, a bright 65-inch Mini LED such as the Hisense U7 or TCL QM7K, or a 75-inch Mini LED such as the Hisense U6. All ten TVs in this guide are 4K and all but the Sony have a 120Hz or faster panel.

OLED or Mini LED?

OLED has perfect blacks, no halos and the widest viewing angles, which makes it the best choice for movies in a dim room. Mini LED gets much brighter, costs less per inch and has no risk of burn-in, which makes it the better choice for sunny living rooms and for sports during the day. Under $1,000, OLED stops at 55 inches.

Which size fits your room?

From about 6 to 8 feet away, 55 inches works well. From 8 to 10 feet, 65 inches feels much more immersive, and beyond 10 feet a 75-inch TV is worth it. Measure your TV stand too: a 75-inch set is about 66 inches wide and its feet often sit near the edges.

Check the HDR formats

Netflix, Disney+ and Apple TV+ use Dolby Vision, while Prime Video uses HDR10+. The Hisense, TCL, Roku and Amazon TVs support both. LG and Sony support Dolby Vision but not HDR10+. Samsung supports HDR10+ but never Dolby Vision. A TV without a format still plays the movie in regular HDR10.

Gaming: refresh rate and HDMI 2.1

For 120fps on a PS5 or Xbox Series X you need a 120Hz panel and an HDMI 2.1 port with VRR. The LG B5 has four HDMI 2.1 inputs. The TCL QM7K has two and the Amazon Omni Mini-LED has one plus an eARC port. For a gaming PC, the 165Hz Hisense U7 is the fastest.

Buying around Black Friday

TV prices are at their lowest of the year in late November, and again before the Super Bowl. Many TVs here launched above $1,000 and reach this budget because they are 2025 models that are being cleared. Compare the price with the previous weeks, and check the exact model number so you do not buy an older or smaller version by mistake.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best TV under $1,000 in 2026?

Based on our comparison of specs, prices and buyer reviews, the LG B5 OLED55B5PUA is the best TV under $1,000. It is a 55-inch OLED with 120Hz, Dolby Vision and four HDMI 2.1 inputs. The Hisense U7 65U75QG is the best value if you want a larger and brighter screen.

Can you get an OLED TV for under $1,000?

Yes, in the 55-inch size. The LG B5 and the Samsung S85F both sell for under $1,000 on Amazon. A 65-inch OLED usually costs $1,000 or more, even on sale.

What is the best 65-inch TV under $1,000?

The Hisense U7 65U75QG. It is a Mini LED TV with a native 165Hz panel, Dolby Vision IQ, HDR10+ and, according to Hisense, up to 3,000 nits of peak brightness. The TCL QM7K is the alternative if black levels matter more to you than maximum brightness.

Can you get a good 75-inch TV for under $1,000?

Yes. The Hisense U6 75U65QF is a 75-inch Mini LED with 144Hz, Dolby Vision IQ and HDR10+ for about $600. It is less bright than the smaller U7, but it is a clear step up from a basic 75-inch LED TV.

Which TV under $1,000 is best for gaming?

For a console, the LG B5 with its four HDMI 2.1 inputs, 120Hz and fast OLED response. For a gaming PC or a bright room, the Hisense U7 with its native 165Hz panel and VRR.

Is OLED burn-in still a problem?

With normal mixed viewing it is rare. OLED TVs shift pixels and refresh the panel automatically to prevent it. If the TV shows the same news ticker or game interface for many hours every day, a Mini LED TV is the safer choice.
