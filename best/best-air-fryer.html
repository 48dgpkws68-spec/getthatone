Buying guide: how to choose the right best air fryer

What size air fryer do you need?

As a rule of thumb, 2 to 4 quarts is enough for one or two people, 5 to 6 quarts suits a family of three or four, and 8 quarts or more is right for larger families. Wide, shallow baskets crisp better than deep ones because food can lie in a single layer. Dual-basket models are great if you often cook a main and a side together.

Basket air fryer or air fryer oven?

Basket air fryers are compact, heat up fast and give the crispiest fries and wings. Air fryer toaster ovens are bigger, also toast, bake and broil, and can fit a pizza or a whole chicken. Choose an oven if you want to replace a toaster and use your full-size oven less. Choose a basket model if crispy snacks and speed come first.

Does an air fryer save energy?

For small and medium portions, yes. An air fryer heats a small space and cooks faster, so it typically uses much less energy than preheating a full-size electric oven. Most air fryers draw 1,500 to 1,800 watts, so plug them into their own outlet and avoid sharing a circuit with a microwave or space heater.

Cleaning and coatings

Most baskets have a nonstick coating and are dishwasher safe, but hand washing with a soft sponge keeps them in good shape longer. Avoid metal utensils. If you prefer to avoid nonstick, look at glass models like the Ninja Crispi or stainless steel interiors in air fryer ovens.

Noise and counter space

Air fryers use a powerful fan, so they make noise similar to a range hood. Newer models with DC motors, like the COSORI TurboBlaze, are quieter. Leave a few inches of space behind and above the unit for hot air to escape, and check the height if you want to use it under wall cabinets.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best air fryer in 2026?

Based on our comparison of specs, prices and buyer reviews, the Ninja DZ201 Foodi DualZone is the best air fryer for most households. Two independent baskets let you cook a full meal at once. The Ninja AF101 is the best value.

Are air fryers healthier than deep frying?

Yes. Air fryers use hot circulating air instead of a bath of oil, so a tablespoon of oil or less is usually enough. That cuts fat and calories compared with deep frying, while still giving a crispy result.

What size air fryer is best for a family of four?

For four people, look for at least 6 quarts in one basket or about 8 quarts across two baskets. The Ninja DZ201 and COSORI TurboBlaze are good choices. Larger families should consider the 10-quart Ninja Double Stack XL.

Can you put aluminum foil or parchment in an air fryer?

Yes, as long as it is weighed down by food and does not block airflow. Never preheat with loose foil or parchment inside, since the fan can blow it into the heating element. Perforated parchment liners are made for air fryers.

Do air fryers use a lot of electricity?

Most air fryers draw between 1,000 and 1,800 watts. Because they cook faster and do not need a long preheat, they usually use less energy per meal than a full-size electric oven.

Which air fryer is easiest to clean?

Models with dishwasher-safe baskets and crisper plates, such as the Ninja AF101 and DZ201, are the easiest. The glass containers of the Ninja Crispi are also simple to clean and have no coating that can wear off.
