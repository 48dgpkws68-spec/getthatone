Both are family board games rated for ages 8 and up. In Ticket to Ride, 2 to 5 players collect train cards and claim routes across North America in 30 to 60 minutes. Kingdomino is a tile laying game for 2 to 4 players that takes about 15 minutes and costs a lot less.

Verdict

Our conclusion

Pick Ticket to Ride if you want the one game everybody likes and you have up to an hour to play. Pick Kingdomino if you want a short, smart game for less money. Keep in mind that counting the score needs some multiplication, so younger players may need help.
