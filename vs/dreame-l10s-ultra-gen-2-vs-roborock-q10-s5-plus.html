Comparison

Dreame L10s Ultra Gen 2 vs Roborock Q10 S5+

Both robots stay under 500 dollars and both reach 10,000 Pa of suction with LiDAR navigation. The difference is the dock: the Dreame empties the bin and washes and dries its spinning mop pads, while the cheaper Roborock only empties the bin and wipes with a vibrating pad.

Who wins in which situation?

You have mostly hard floors and want real moppingDreame L10s Ultra Gen 2

Two spinning pads, one of which extends to the edges, are washed and dried by the dock.

You never want to rinse a mop pad by handDreame L10s Ultra Gen 2

The base station washes the pads and dries them with warm air.

You mainly need vacuuming on carpetRoborock Q10 S5+

It has the same 10,000 Pa of suction and an anti-tangle brush for less money.

You want the easiest appRoborock Q10 S5+

The Roborock app offers room by room settings and no-go zones, while the Dreame app takes getting used to.

Verdict

Our conclusion

Pick the Dreame L10s Ultra Gen 2 if mopping matters and you have room for a large dock. Pick the Roborock Q10 S5+ if you mostly vacuum, want to spend about a hundred dollars less and do not mind rinsing the mop pad yourself.
