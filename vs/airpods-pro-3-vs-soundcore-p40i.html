Apple AirPods Pro 3 vs Soundcore P40i by Anker

The AirPods Pro 3 and the Soundcore P40i both offer adaptive noise cancelling, but they sit at opposite ends of the price range. Apple's buds are the premium, feature-packed choice for iPhone owners, while Anker's Soundcore P40i costs a fraction and has longer battery life.

Who wins in which situation?

iPhone owner who wants everythingApple AirPods Pro 3

Heart rate sensing, hearing test, hearing aid mode and seamless switching between Apple devices only work properly with Apple.

Tight budgetSoundcore P40i by Anker

It has adaptive noise cancelling for about a quarter of the price or less, and a phone-stand case.

Android phoneSoundcore P40i by Anker

It works with iPhone and Android alike, and offers multipoint pairing. The AirPods lose most of their smart features on Android.

Best noise cancelling and workoutsApple AirPods Pro 3

Noise cancellation is stronger than the P40i's and the IP57 rating beats the P40i's IPX5 for sweat and rain.

Verdict

Our conclusion

iPhone owners who want the best noise cancelling, call quality and Apple features should buy the AirPods Pro 3. Everyone else, especially students, commuters and Android users, will get more for their money from the Soundcore P40i, which has longer battery life and respectable ANC. Don't pay for the Apple extras if you won't use them.
