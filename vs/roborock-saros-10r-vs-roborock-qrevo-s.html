Roborock Saros 10R vs Roborock Qrevo S

Who wins in which situation?

Tight budgetRoborock Qrevo S

It gives you auto-empty, mop washing and drying at roughly half the price or less, with reliable LiDAR mapping.

Homes with cords, socks and petsRoborock Saros 10R

The AI camera spots cables and pet waste and steers around them. The Qrevo S has no camera, so you tidy up first.

Rugs and carpetRoborock Saros 10R

Its mops lift 0.8 inches and can be dropped off at the dock, compared with 0.4 inches on the Qrevo S.

First robot vacuumRoborock Qrevo S

The Qrevo S is simple, has a strong app with room-by-room settings and costs far less to try.

Verdict

Our conclusion

The Roborock Saros 10R is the better robot, with stronger suction, a camera, hot water mop washing and a body slim enough to fit under low furniture, but it is priced like a flagship. For most first-time buyers with mainly hard floors and low-pile carpet, the Qrevo S does the essentials for much less. Choose the Saros if your home has lots of clutter or thick rugs.
