Verdict

Our conclusion

If you run a heater every night in a bedroom or office, the Dreo DR-HSH012 earns its higher price with precise digital control and stronger safety features. For occasional use or a guest room, the Amazon Basics does the core job just as quietly. Both take a while to warm a room, as all oil heaters do. Most buyers who want set-and-forget comfort should pick the Dreo.
