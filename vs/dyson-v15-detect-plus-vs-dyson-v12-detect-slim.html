Who wins in which situation?

Thick carpet and petsDyson V15 Detect Plus

With 240 AW against 150 AW and the anti-tangle Digital Motorbar head, the V15 pulls more dirt and hair out of carpet.

Stairs, curtains and ceilingsDyson V12 Detect Slim

At about 5.2 pounds it is much lighter than the 6.8 pound V15, which buyers with wrist or shoulder issues notice right away.

Lots of debris per sessionDyson V15 Detect Plus

Its 0.2 gallon bin holds about twice as much as the V12's, so you stop to empty it far less often.

Small apartment with hard floorsDyson V12 Detect Slim

You still get the laser head and dust sensor, the battery clicks out for a spare and it costs less.

Verdict

Our conclusion

Get the Dyson V15 Detect Plus if you have carpet, shedding pets or a larger home, since the extra suction and bigger bin make a real difference there. If you live in an apartment with mostly hard floors and rugs, the V12 Detect Slim gives you the same smart features in a lighter body for less money. Weight is the main reason to choose the V12.
