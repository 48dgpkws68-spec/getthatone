MSI Katana 15 HX B14WEK RTX 5050 vs Lenovo LOQ 15 (Ryzen 5 7235HS, RTX 4050)

Two 15.6 inch gaming laptops under $1,000 with a full HD screen at 144Hz and 16GB of DDR5. The MSI Katana 15 HX has the newer RTX 5050 with 8GB of video memory, a 16 core Intel Core i7-14650HX and a 1TB SSD, and it sits right at the top of the budget. The Lenovo LOQ 15 has an RTX 4050 with 6GB, a four core Ryzen 5 7235HS and a 512GB SSD, and costs clearly less.

You want the laptop to keep up with new games for yearsMSI Katana 15 HX B14WEK RTX 5050

The RTX 5050 has 8GB of video memory and supports DLSS 4.

You also stream, edit video or install many big gamesMSI Katana 15 HX B14WEK RTX 5050

You want to stay well below $1,000Lenovo LOQ 15 (Ryzen 5 7235HS, RTX 4050)

It costs clearly less and the RTX 4050 still runs 1080p games well with DLSS.
