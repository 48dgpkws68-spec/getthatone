Verdict

Our conclusion

Choose the LG C5 if you watch mostly in the evening, want the best contrast and wide viewing angles, or connect several gaming devices. Choose the TCL QM7K if your room is bright, you watch a lot of daytime TV, or you would rather spend the difference on a good soundbar. Both are excellent 65-inch TVs.
