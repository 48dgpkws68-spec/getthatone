LG C5 OLED55C5PUA vs TCL QM6K 55QM6K

The LG C5 and the TCL QM6K are two of the most talked-about 55-inch sets, and the price gap between them is big enough to make people stop and think. Both run at 144Hz and look great with games and movies. The core difference is panel tech: the C5 is an OLED with perfect blacks, while the QM6K is a Mini LED that goes much brighter for far less money.

Who wins in which situation?

Tight budgetTCL QM6K 55QM6K

The TCL costs a fraction of the LG and still brings a bright Mini LED picture, 144Hz and both Dolby Vision and HDR10+.

Dark room movie nightsLG C5 OLED55C5PUA

OLED turns off individual pixels, so blacks are perfect and viewing angles stay wide. The TCL can show some blooming around bright objects.

Sunny living roomTCL QM6K 55QM6K

The Mini LED backlight gets bright enough to fight afternoon glare, which is the one area where the LG is weaker.

Serious console gamingLG C5 OLED55C5PUA

All four HDMI ports on the LG are HDMI 2.1 at 144Hz, while the TCL has two, which can get tight with a console, a PC and a soundbar.

Verdict

Our conclusion

If you watch mostly in the evening or care about the best possible picture and gaming ports, the LG C5 is the TV to buy and worth the extra money. If you want the most screen for the least cash, or your room gets a lot of daylight, the TCL QM6K is a very easy recommendation. Plan on a soundbar with the TCL, since its speakers are thin.
