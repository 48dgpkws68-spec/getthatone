Who wins in which situation?

Heating a living room or basementVornado VH200 Whole Room Vortex Heater

The vortex airflow mixes warm air through the whole room, so it suits medium to large spaces better than a directional heater.

Bedroom or dormDreo Atom One Space Heater

It is compact, quiet and has a 12-hour timer, though the bright display bothers some people at night.

Exact temperature controlDreo Atom One Space Heater

The digital thermostat works in 1°F steps and eco mode trims output as the room nears the set point.

Long-term durabilityVornado VH200 Whole Room Vortex Heater

Vornado backs the VH200 with a 5-year warranty and it has been a long-running Amazon bestseller.

Verdict

Our conclusion

Pick the Vornado VH200 if you want even heat in a bigger room and value the longer warranty. Pick the Dreo Atom One if you are heating a small room, want a digital thermostat and prefer to spend less. The Dreo is best for rooms up to about 200 square feet, so don't expect it to warm a big open space.
