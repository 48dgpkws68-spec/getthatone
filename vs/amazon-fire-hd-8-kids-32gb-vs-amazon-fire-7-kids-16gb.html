Amazon Fire HD 8 Kids 32GB vs Amazon Fire 7 Kids 16GB

Both are Amazon kids tablets listed for ages 3 to 7, with a kid-proof case with stand, the same parental controls and a 2-year worry-free guarantee. The Fire HD 8 Kids has an 8-inch HD screen, 32GB of storage, up to 13 hours of battery life and one year of Amazon Kids+. The Fire 7 Kids has a 7-inch screen, 16GB of storage, up to 10 hours of battery life and six months of Kids+, and it costs less.

Verdict

Our conclusion

Pick the Fire HD 8 Kids if the tablet will be used daily: the 1280 x 800 screen is sharper, it has 3GB of RAM, twice the storage, a longer battery life and a full year of Kids+. Pick the Fire 7 Kids if you want a low-cost first tablet or a second one for the car, and you accept a 1024 x 600 screen and 16GB of storage that you will probably expand with a microSD card.
