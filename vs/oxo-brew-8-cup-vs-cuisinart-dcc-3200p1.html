Verdict

Our conclusion

If you drink a couple of cups a morning and care about flavor, the OXO Brew 8-Cup is the better machine and its thermal carafe is a real perk. If you brew a full pot every day for a family or office and want to spend less, the Cuisinart PerfecTemp gets the job done. Just don't leave coffee on its warming plate for too long.
