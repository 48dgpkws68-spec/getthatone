Three tips for buying gifts

Order early

Delivery times get longer from late November. Order by mid December to be on the safe side.

Check the return policy

Amazon often extends the return window for purchases made in the holiday season, so the recipient can still exchange a gift. The exact terms are on the product page.

Pick a model, not a brand

A well-known brand is not always the best gift. Our guides show which model offers the most for the price.
